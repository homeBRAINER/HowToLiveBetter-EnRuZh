## Live better. Spend your resources wisely.

A guide to longevity and disease prevention, emergencies and first aid, saving money, avoiding scams and legal trouble, unemployment, business, relationships, parenting, travel and practical skills.

The original contains **498 recommendations in 31 chapters**. Each explains what it costs, what you may gain, how strong the evidence is, and where the claim comes from. Sources are journal articles and official documents.

> **Translation in progress.** This release introduces the English and Russian welcome pages and the language selector. The chapters and source-verification records are still in Chinese. Chapter links below open that original text. Laws, benefits, prices and emergency services refer to China unless a passage explicitly says otherwise.

## How to read this guide

- **Browse by topic:** start with a chapter below. Recommendations within each chapter are ordered by the author's assessment of benefit relative to cost.
- **Filter the collection:** the search page combines chapter, evidence grade, expected benefit, money, time and willpower. Multiple choices within one group mean “or”; different groups are combined with “and”. Until the chapters are translated, searching their text requires Chinese terms or the original scientific abbreviations.
- **Read the plain-language explanation:** each recommendation has a short explanation alongside the detailed results, numbers and confidence intervals.
- **Check uncertainty:** disputed evidence and claims still awaiting verification remain explicitly marked. A translation does not constitute a new verification of the sources.

## Four resources

| Resource | What the guide tries to protect |
| --- | --- |
| Lifespan | More years of life; fewer avoidable deaths. |
| Time and energy | Less wasted time, attention and physical effort. |
| Money | Fewer unnecessary expenses and better use of limited funds. |
| Personal freedom | Avoiding legal consequences caused by not knowing the rules. |

Each recommendation asks two questions: **What does it cost? What do you get back?** Mortality, time, money and legal consequences are measured separately, not converted into a single score.

The original also distinguishes who benefits: yourself; your spouse and direct relatives; friends, colleagues and other relatives; strangers. This is the author's framework for expected personal returns, including reciprocity and risks. Benefits for different groups are not added together. Helping strangers is presented as an individual decision, with both potential benefits and risks described.

## Evidence grades

These are the author's categories, not the GRADE framework.

| Grade | Meaning in this guide |
| --- | --- |
| A | Quantifiable findings from a meta-analysis, large cohort or randomized controlled trial; in legal and benefits chapters, verified official figures. |
| B | Research support with limited quantification, or a small or single study; some official provisions whose amounts depend on locality. |
| C | Author experience or general consensus without direct supporting research. |

The original reports **323 A-grade, 126 B-grade and 49 C-grade recommendations**, **45 disputed entries** and **39 TODO markers**. Evidence grades, caveats and links will be retained in translation. Unverified claims will not silently become verified ones.

## Benefit relative to cost

Evidence strength answers “How well is this claim supported?” The cost–benefit category answers a different question: “Is it worth the resources?” It is an **author judgment**, independent of the evidence grade.

| Category | Rule used in the original |
| --- | --- |
| Very high | Large benefit and zero cost across all three cost dimensions. |
| High | Large benefit with low costs, or medium benefit with zero costs. |
| Moderate | Other combinations; this does not mean the action is not worth taking. |

Benefit magnitude is assessed within its own domain: relative mortality reduction; money saved; legal consequences avoided; or time saved. Do not compare a 12% reduction in mortality with saving 500 yuan a year as though they were the same measure.
