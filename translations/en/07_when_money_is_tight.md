# 7. When money is tight

> Partial translation: the introduction and entries 1–6 of 22. Entries 7–22 are still available in [Chinese](../../book/07-没钱的时候怎么活.md). The numbered provisions describe China; regional notes give separate US and European starting points. Legal clarifications are labeled. Regional links checked September 21, 2026; eligibility still depends on the current local rules.

This chapter measures money and social protection: how much you can receive, which charges can be waived, what services are available, and who qualifies. It does not convert these benefits into years of life. Policies change over time and differ by place; use the amounts and thresholds published locally for the relevant year. The order favors help that costs nothing, involves little paperwork, and can arrive quickly. Measures requiring long-term eligibility or varying greatly by region come later, followed by experience-based suggestions.

### 1. If you lose your job, apply online for unemployment insurance
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No application fee in China. Apply through the Zhangshang 12333 app, the electronic social security card mini-program in WeChat or Alipay, or the national social insurance public service platform, using your social security card or identity document. Most areas do not require an office visit. Unemployment registration is part of eligibility; ask whether it can be processed with the claim.
- In plain language: In China, you may qualify if you and your employer paid contributions for at least 1 year, you became unemployed involuntarily, and you register as unemployed and seek work. Contributions for 1 to under 5 years allow up to 12 months of benefits; 5 to under 10 years, up to 18 months; and 10 years or more, up to 24 months. Provinces set the amount. The 1999 regulations place it below the local minimum wage and above the urban minimum living allowance. The unemployment insurance fund also pays employee medical insurance contributions while you receive benefits. Simply choosing to quit normally does not qualify; legally recognized departures caused by an employer's violations can be different.
- Benefit: In China, eligibility requires at least 1 year of employer and employee contributions, involuntary unemployment, unemployment registration, and a willingness to seek work. The maximum duration is 12 months after 1 to under 5 contribution years, 18 months after 5 to under 10 years, and 24 months after at least 10 years. Provincial governments set the payment: the regulations effective from 1999 specify an amount below the local minimum wage and above the urban minimum living allowance. National policy calls for gradually raising it to 90% of the minimum wage. This is a policy target, not a promise of an identical payment everywhere.
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: State Council (1999). Unemployment Insurance Regulations, Order No. 258, Articles 14, 17, and 18. <https://xzfg.moj.gov.cn/front/law/detail?LawID=517>; Ministry of Human Resources and Social Security (MOHRSS). National government services platform: guide to claiming unemployment insurance benefits. <https://www.12333.gov.cn/portal/common/bszn/sydysl?pfaId=202105281700000004>; National Development and Reform Commission, Employment Department (2022). Gradually raising unemployment benefits to 90% of the minimum wage. <https://www.ndrc.gov.cn/fggz/jyysr/jysrsbxf/202206/t20220627_1328819.html>
- Notes: An ordinary voluntary resignation does not count as involuntary unemployment. Employee medical insurance contributions are paid through unemployment insurance during the benefit period. Amounts vary considerably: check the rules where you were insured. The 90% target is attributed to MOHRSS guidance from 2017; its document number remains unverified because the ministry and central government copies could not be opened during the earlier source check. **Legal clarification:** qualifying departures include certain cases of leaving because an employer withheld pay or imposed unlawful working conditions; see the [claim rules](https://www.mohrss.gov.cn/xxgk2020/fdzdgknr/zcfg/bmgz/202011/t20201102_394633.html). Registration and claiming can be handled together under the [registration guidance](https://rsj.beijing.gov.cn/xxgk/zfxxgk/zdlygk/yhys/xgzc/202311/P020231117343285173532.pdf). Do not assume you need a separate preliminary visit. **US/Europe:** in the US, start with your [state unemployment program](https://www.usa.gov/unemployment-benefits), usually in the state where you worked. Eligibility, amounts, and duration depend on state rules. In Europe, contact the relevant national employment service; [Your Europe](https://europa.eu/youreurope/citizens/work/social-security-and-benefits/cannot-find-work-abroad/faq/index_en.htm) explains cross-border situations. Chinese contribution periods, payment levels, and medical coverage do not transfer to these systems.

### 2. If wages go unpaid, use labor inspection and labor arbitration: both are free in China
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=中 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No complaint or arbitration fee. Gather your contract, pay slips, attendance records, bank transfers, and relevant messages. Allow roughly 1–3 visits to the labor authority or arbitration commission, though the actual process varies. The usual arbitration limitation period is 1 year; unpaid wage claims during an ongoing employment relationship have a special exception. After employment ends, normally apply within 1 year. Rules on interruption and suspension of the limitation period can also matter.
- In plain language: In China, call 12333 or complain to the local labor inspectorate. The normal investigation deadline is 60 working days after a case is opened. Inspectors can order payment; failure to meet that order can lead to additional compensation of 50%–100% of the amount owed. In 2024, inspection recovered 8.65 billion CNY for 654,000 workers. Arbitration is another free route, with a normal 45-day deadline after acceptance. Certain awards for amounts no greater than 12 times the local monthly minimum wage are final for the employer in ordinary proceedings, although a limited court challenge remains possible. These are procedural deadlines, not a guarantee that money will arrive within a few months. An employer with no recoverable assets may still fail to pay after you win. Keep the usual 1-year deadline after leaving in mind; you do not have to finish an inspection complaint before applying for arbitration.
- Benefit: In China, start by calling 12333 or contacting labor inspection. An investigation normally takes up to 60 working days after the case is opened, extendable by 30 working days for a complex case. Inspectors can order payment and, after noncompliance, additional compensation of 50%–100%. In 2024 they handled 137,000 cases and recovered 8.65 billion CNY for 654,000 workers. If the debt is clear and supported by an acknowledgment or payroll records, a payment order from a basic-level court may be available; the statutory issue period is 15 days after acceptance when the legal conditions are met. Labor arbitration normally concludes within 45 days, with a possible 15-day extension. Provisional enforcement of wage claims may be available if its conditions are met. Certain claims not exceeding 12 times the local monthly minimum wage qualify for a final award, without an ordinary employer lawsuit to reopen the merits. In 2024, labor and personnel dispute bodies handled 4.257 million cases, closed 4.156 million, and reported 93.47 billion CNY in amounts involved in closed cases; mediation success was 79.6%, the arbitration case-closure rate 98.2%, and the reported arbitration finality rate 73.7%. Cases that proceed to court can take longer: the ordinary first-instance period is 6 months, or 3 months under the simplified procedure, followed by a 3-month appeal period, subject to applicable extensions and excluded periods. This is why some cases take half a year or a year. In 2024, courts concluded 88,000 wage-recovery cases, recovered 28.54 billion CNY, and sentenced 998 people for criminal refusal to pay labor remuneration.
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: MOHRSS (2025). Labor Contract Law, Articles 30 and 85. <https://fgk.chinatax.gov.cn/zcfgk/c100009/c5193025/content.html>; Standing Committee of the National People's Congress (amended 2023). - Notes: The 98.2% closure rate describes completion of proceedings, not successful collection. If the employer has no assets or has disappeared, an award may remain unpaid. The Supreme People's Court reported 2.3 trillion CNY recovered through enforcement in 2024 across all case types and described a high enforcement-completion rate; the cited report does not give the share of unpaid-wage claimants who actually received their money. The bulletin does not define the 73.7% arbitration finality indicator. Reading it as the share of awards that never proceeded to court is an interpretation, not a confirmed definition. Chinese criminal thresholds cover nonpayment to one worker for at least 3 months above a locally set threshold within 5,000–20,000 CNY, or to at least 10 workers above a total threshold within 30,000–100,000 CNY. **Legal clarification:** crossing a threshold and ignoring a payment order are not the entire offense: evading payment, for example by hiding assets or fleeing, or refusing despite having the ability to pay also matters. Explain these facts when complaining. Construction migrant workers have additional safeguards, including dedicated wage accounts and general-contractor payment obligations in specified circumstances; approach the general contractor as well as the immediate subcontractor. Migrant workers seeking legal aid to recover wages are exempt from financial-hardship verification. The Labor Contract Law and Civil Procedure Law citations use official tax authority and Guizhou market regulator reproductions because the National People's Congress pages were unavailable in the earlier source check; the Criminal Law amendment required an alternative retrieval method after the direct request failed. **Legal clarification:** Article 49 allows an employer to seek cancellation of a final arbitration award within 30 days on specified grounds; “final” does not eliminate every challenge. **US/Europe:** US minimum-wage and overtime complaints can go to the [Wage and Hour Division](https://www.dol.gov/sites/dolgov/files/WHD/legacy/files/HowToFileAComplaint-eng.pdf); its assistance is free and confidential. Other wage claims may belong with a state agency or court. European procedures and limitation periods depend on the country; obtain local advice promptly rather than applying China's 1-year rule.

### 3. If you cannot afford a lawyer, check whether legal aid covers your case
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: Legal aid is free to its beneficiaries in China.
- In plain language: Wage claims, maintenance, social insurance or minimum living allowance disputes, and civil claims arising from helping someone in danger can fall within China's legal aid system. Call 12348 or contact a local legal aid center. Since 2022, you can truthfully explain your financial hardship without returning to your hometown for a certificate. In specified criminal cases, including an unrepresented defendant who may face a death sentence or who is blind, deaf, unable to speak, or a minor, a lawyer must be assigned regardless of income. **Legal clarification:** coverage of a civil case type alone does not make every applicant eligible; financial conditions or a statutory exemption still apply. Financial declarations can be checked, and the mandatory criminal-defense categories are broader than these examples.
- Benefit: The Chinese regulations list civil legal aid for state compensation; social insurance or minimum living allowances; pensions and relief payments; support for older relatives, children, or other dependents; wages; and civil rights arising from brave assistance to others. Criminal legal aid includes defendants facing a possible death sentence and blind, deaf, non-speaking, or minor defendants, regardless of means, when they lack defense counsel. The current Legal Aid Law also expressly includes qualifying workplace-injury compensation cases.
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: State Council (2003). Legal Aid Regulations, Order No. 385, Articles 10, 11, and 12. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/2008-03/28/content_6944.htm>; Ministry of Justice officials (2021). Questions and answers on implementing the Legal Aid Law. <http://www.gov.cn/zhengce/2021-12/29/content_5665114.htm>
- Notes: The Legal Aid Law effective January 1, 2022 replaced the requirement to submit a financial-hardship certificate with a truthful account of financial circumstances; there is no need to return to the place of household registration just to obtain that certificate. Contact a local legal aid center, public legal services center, or 12348. The [2021 law](https://www.npc.gov.cn/npc/c2/c30834/202108/t20210820_313079.html) distinguishes ordinary financial eligibility, exemptions, and verification. **US/Europe:** the [Legal Services Corporation directory](https://www.lsc.gov/about-lsc/what-legal-aid/i-need-legal-help) helps find US civil legal aid providers for people on low incomes; coverage depends on eligibility and the case. European rules differ by country: the [European e-Justice portal's guide to France](https://e-justice.europa.eu/topics/taking-legal-action/legal-aid/fr_en), for example, explains full or partial assistance. Neither system should be read as a promise of a free lawyer for every dispute.

### 4. If you have nowhere to go, ask a shelter about food, accommodation, and help getting home
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No charge at eligible Chinese relief stations. Go to a local relief management station, or ask the police or civil affairs department for a referral. Give accurate details about your identity and belongings.
- In plain language: China's relief stations can provide meals, a place to sleep, transfer to medical care for an acute illness, help contacting family, and travel vouchers if you cannot afford to return home. A stay normally lasts no more than 10 days. Entering and leaving are voluntary: this is assistance, not detention, and it does not provide cash. Travel is to your place of household registration or residence, so decide where you need to return. **Eligibility clarification:** this scheme is for destitute people within the regulations' scope; it is not a general free hotel or travel service.
- Benefit: Under China's nationwide scheme introduced in 2003, stations provide food meeting hygiene requirements, accommodation, hospital transfer for sudden illness at the station, help contacting relatives or an employer, and train or boat travel vouchers for people without the fare to return to their registered home or residence. Assistance normally lasts no more than 10 days; it can be extended while awaiting collection, treatment, or placement. Police and other public officials who encounter destitute people living by begging while on duty must tell them about help. They must guide and escort disabled people, minors, older people, and people with limited mobility as provided by the rules.
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: State Council (2003). Measures for Assistance to Destitute Vagrants and Beggars in Urban Areas, Order No. 381, Articles 5, 6, and 7. <https://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2003/content_62246.htm>; Ministry of Civil Affairs (2003). Implementing Rules, Order No. 24, Articles 11 and 12. <https://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2003/content_62510.htm>
- Notes: Seeking help and leaving are voluntary. The service provides basic food and accommodation, not cash. Travel vouchers are for the place of household registration or residence; consider your destination before applying. **US/Europe:** use the [US government's emergency housing guide](https://www.usa.gov/emergency-housing-assistance) to find local shelter services. In France, call **115** for emergency shelter assistance: the [official service guide](https://www.service-public.gouv.fr/particuliers/vosdroits/F2003) describes this free, round-the-clock line. It is a French service, not a Europe-wide number. Bed availability, admission rules, and help with travel vary; a call does not guarantee an immediate place.

### 5. In an emergency, call for help even if you cannot pay
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: Calling China's 120 emergency number is free. Ambulance services and treatment can still generate charges; a free call does not mean all care is free.
- In plain language: In China, emergency centers and hospitals must not refuse, divert, or delay care for a critically ill or seriously injured person because of payment. Violations can lead to an order to correct the practice and disciplinary action against responsible staff. The emergency medical assistance fund can cover qualifying emergency expenses for unidentified patients or people unable to pay, after the applicable funding checks. Call 120 even if you have no money. The fund covers the emergency phase; later inpatient care is a separate matter.
- Benefit: Chinese emergency centers and network hospitals must not refuse or delay prehospital emergency care over fees. Violations can require correction and disciplinary action. Medical institutions of all types and levels must not refuse, divert, or delay treatment for acute, critical illness or injury. The emergency medical assistance system covers qualifying emergency costs for unidentified patients or patients unable to pay, under nationwide measures introduced in 2013 and 2014.
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: General Office of the State Council (2013). Guidance on establishing the emergency medical assistance system, Guo Ban Fa [2013] No. 15. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/2013-03/01/content_6069.htm>; National Health and Family Planning Commission (2013). Measures for Prehospital Emergency Medical Care, Order No. 3, Articles 25 and 37. <https://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2014/content_2580977.htm>
- Notes: The fund covers the emergency phase. Later hospitalization and treatment may require personal payment, medical insurance, or medical assistance; see [entry 11 of this chapter](../../book/07-没钱的时候怎么活.md). **Cross-reference correction:** medical assistance is entry 11; entry 8 concerns the minimum living allowance. The [Chinese health authority clarification](https://www.nhc.gov.cn/yzygj/c100068/202101/e7b3172719684ece805d86f91e20ed27.shtml) explains that identified patients first use applicable insurance and other payment channels; the fund can cover the remaining shortfall. Do not delay emergency care because you fear the bill. **US/Europe:** call **911 in the US** or **112 in the EU**. [CMS explains](https://www.cms.gov/medicare/regulations-guidance/legislation/emergency-medical-treatment-labor-act) that Medicare-participating hospitals offering emergency services must provide an appropriate screening examination and, for an emergency medical condition, stabilizing treatment or an appropriate transfer regardless of ability to pay. This does not erase the bill or make every ambulance trip free. The [EU's 112 number](https://europa.eu/youreurope/citizens/travel/security-and-emergencies/emergency/index_en.htm) is free to call; treatment charges and coverage depend on the country and your circumstances.

### 6. Start with free public employment services and casual-work listings
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No charge for the specified public services in China. Register with a county-level or higher public employment service, employment center, human resources market, or local casual-work market. Call 12333 to find the right office.
- In plain language: China's public employment services must offer free job matching, career guidance, policy advice, and employment and unemployment registration; the law prohibits them from conducting commercial activities. Registration and publication of casual-work vacancies and jobseeker information are also free. Arrange unemployment registration while you are there: it matters for unemployment insurance and recognition as someone facing difficulty finding work. Ask whether registration can be processed with your benefit application.
- Benefit: Chinese law provides free employment-policy advice, vacancy and labor-market information, career guidance, job placement, employment and unemployment registration, and assistance for people facing employment difficulties. Public employment institutions must not engage in commercial activities. Registration and publication of casual-work recruitment and jobseeker information are free public employment information services. Workers facing long waits for assignments or coming from low-income households should receive additional assistance under the national policy.
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: Standing Committee of the National People's Congress (2007, amended 2015). Employment Promotion Law, Article 35. <https://www.gov.cn/guoqing/2021-10/29/content_5647636.htm>; MOHRSS and four other departments (2022). Opinions on strengthening casual-work markets and improving recruitment services, Ren She Bu Fa [2022] No. 38. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/2022-07/09/content_5700177.htm>
- Notes: Unemployment registration is part of eligibility for unemployment insurance and relevant employment-difficulty support. Arrange it with the office; a separate preliminary trip is not always necessary. For daily-paid casual work, agree and check the pay on site, and keep contact details and records. **US/Europe:** [American Job Centers](https://cloudfront.careeronestop.org/LocalHelp/AmericanJobCenters/american-job-centers.aspx) offer free help with job searching and career planning; facilities and programs vary by location. [EURES](https://eures.europa.eu/jobseekers_en) offers European job information and advice, with most services free. Check the particular service before agreeing to a fee, and distinguish public employment support from a private recruitment agency.
