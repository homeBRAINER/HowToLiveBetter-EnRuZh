# 15. Renting and buying a home

The outcome here is money. When renting, a deposit or an agent can cost you more than expected. When buying, mortgage interest is a major expense, and the seller may know things about the property that you do not.

> Translation complete: the introduction and all eight items have been checked against the Chinese chapter. Rules in other countries are identified separately.

### 1. Put the deposit amount, return date and permitted deductions in the lease
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No purchase is needed. Allow about ten extra minutes when signing; the harder part may be raising the issue with the landlord.
- In plain language: Before you sign, make sure the lease says how much you pay, when you get it back and what can be deducted. Ask for a written explanation and evidence for any deduction when you move out. In China, a landlord cannot make a deduction outside the agreed circumstances without a justified reason.
- Benefit: Article 10 of China's Housing Rental Regulation, in force since 15 September 2025, requires the lease to set out the deposit amount, return time and circumstances for deductions. Outside those circumstances, the landlord may not deduct without a justified reason. Written terms and evidence of the home's condition make it easier to check a disputed calculation.
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: [China's State Council, Housing Rental Regulation, Order No. 812 (2025; effective 2025-09-15), Article 10](https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202507/content_7032956.htm); [China's Ministry of Justice, official regulation database, Article 10](https://xzfg.moj.gov.cn/front/law/detail?LawID=1774); [New York State Homes and Community Renewal, security-deposit fact sheet (November 2025)](https://hcr.ny.gov/fact-sheet-9); [France's official public-service guide, rental deposits](https://www.service-public.fr/particuliers/vosdroits/F31269).
- Notes: On moving-out day, photograph or film the home with the landlord if possible. Record the water, electricity and gas meter readings, walls and floors; keep the inventory, lease and proof of payment. In China, complain about an unjustified deduction to the local housing authority, or consider a small claim; see [chapter 8](08_protect_your_rights_and_property.md). Laws differ elsewhere. In New York State, a typical residential security deposit is capped at one month's rent, and the landlord generally has 14 days after you leave to return the balance and itemize any deductions. In France, the amount and return deadline depend on the tenancy and the entry and exit inspections; a deposit for an unfurnished primary residence is generally capped at one month's rent before charges. These are examples, not rules for every US state or European country.

### 2. If a landlord cuts off utilities, changes the locks or threatens you to make you leave, call for help and keep evidence
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: Calling the police requires no purchase. Act immediately if you face a threat.
- In plain language: A landlord should not use threats, violence or other unlawful methods to force you to end the lease or leave. Cutting off utilities or changing locks can be signs of such pressure. If you are threatened or locked out, call the police, preserve messages and photos, and do not confront anyone physically.
- Benefit: In China, Article 12 of the Housing Rental Regulation forbids a landlord to use violence, threats or other unlawful methods to force a tenant to end the lease or vacate. It also requires notice of a lawful termination and reasonable time to leave. A police incident record and other evidence may help if you later seek compensation, though a call alone does not guarantee it.
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: [China's State Council, Housing Rental Regulation, Order No. 812 (2025; effective 2025-09-15), Article 12](https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202507/content_7032956.htm); [China's Ministry of Justice, official regulation database, Article 12](https://xzfg.moj.gov.cn/front/law/detail?LawID=1774); [New York Attorney General, unlawful evictions](https://ag.ny.gov/resources/government-organizations/law-enforcement-guidance/unlawful-evictions); [UK government, harassment and illegal evictions in England](https://www.gov.uk/private-renting-evictions/harassment-and-illegal-evictions).
- Notes: In China, also notify the local housing-rental authority. In New York, changing a lock without giving a lawful occupant a key and interrupting essential services can amount to unlawful eviction under state rules. In England, the government lists cutting off electricity and threatening to change locks as possible harassment; contact the police if violence or threats are involved or someone tries to remove you without the proper court process. These examples do not establish a single rule for the whole US or Europe. Record when services stopped, your contact with the utility provider and the condition of the lock. Do not try to force your way back in.

### 3. In China, pay rent and the deposit to the landlord named in your lease, not the broker
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No purchase is needed. Check the recipient before paying; that takes a few minutes. The hard part may be resisting a broker who asks you to transfer the money to them.
- In plain language: Chinese rules prohibit a real-estate brokerage from collecting or paying rent and deposits on behalf of the parties. Pay the landlord or rental company named in the lease, after checking who is entitled to rent out the property. If the money passes through a broker who disappears, you could face a dispute about whether rent was paid, as well as the loss of your money or home.
- Benefit: Article 25 of China's Housing Rental Regulation expressly bans housing-rental brokerages from receiving and disbursing rent and deposits for others. Paying the contracted landlord removes one intermediary from the money trail, though it cannot prevent every form of fraud.
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: [China's State Council, Housing Rental Regulation, Order No. 812 (2025; effective 2025-09-15), Article 25](https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202507/content_7032956.htm); [China's Ministry of Justice, official regulation database, Article 25](https://xzfg.moj.gov.cn/front/law/detail?LawID=1774); [UK government, client-money protection for letting agents in England](https://www.gov.uk/client-money-protection-scheme-property-agents); [UK government, tenancy-deposit protection](https://www.gov.uk/tenancy-deposit-protection).
- Notes: Check whether the payee's name matches the owner on the title document. If someone else asks to receive the money, find out why and keep written authority. In China a broker may charge for its brokerage service; it may not receive and disburse the rent or deposit for the contracting parties. The Chinese ban is not a rule for all US states or European countries. In England, a letting agent may hold client money under a client-money protection scheme, and a tenancy deposit has its own protection rules. Verify the payee, the scheme and a receipt before you transfer money.

### 4. Before renting from a company, check its regulated rental-funds account and avoid a year's prepayment for a small discount
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=中 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: Checking the account is free and takes around fifteen minutes. Reading the payment and loan terms closely takes some attention.
- In plain language: A discount for paying a full year ahead may be too small to justify the risk. If a company rents a home from its owner and sublets it to you, then fails soon after you pay, your deposit and more than ten months of prepaid rent may be at risk. If a separate loan paid the company, you may still owe the lender. Before signing, look for the regulated account the company must disclose under the applicable Chinese rule, and check whether any loan is bundled with the lease.
- Benefit: Article 19 of China's Housing Rental Regulation requires companies that sublet homes as a business to establish and publicly disclose an account supervised for rental funds, and to receive and pay rental funds through it. Subletting here means renting a home from its owner and then renting it to you. Checking that account and limiting advance payments reduce the amount exposed if a company fails; the regulation does not quantify how much money you will save.
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: [China's State Council, Housing Rental Regulation, Order No. 812 (2025; effective 2025-09-15), Article 19](https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202507/content_7032956.htm); [China's Ministry of Justice, official regulation database, Article 19](https://xzfg.moj.gov.cn/front/law/detail?LawID=1774).
- Notes: Check whether signing the lease also means taking a loan through an installment app. If the lender pays a year's rent to the company at once while you repay it each month, the company's failure may leave the loan outstanding. Do not sign until you understand cancellation rights, repayment duties and who bears the loss. China's account rule does not automatically apply in the US or Europe. Check the rules and safeguards where you rent, and avoid large prepayments without reliable protection.

### 5. If your rented home is sold, check your rights before agreeing to move
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: Checking your lease and any notice costs nothing. Keep proof of when you moved in.
- In plain language: In China, selling a home does not by itself end a lease while you lawfully occupy it under that lease. The new owner cannot make you leave early or renegotiate the rent solely because they bought the property. Other lawful grounds for ending a lease still matter. Keep your lease, payment records and proof of the move-in date; check your local rules before handing over the keys.
- Benefit: Article 725 of China's Civil Code keeps a lease effective if ownership changes while the tenant possesses the property under the lease. Records of the agreement and lawful occupation help establish whether that condition is met.
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: [China's Civil Code (2020), Article 725, source link](https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/fl/202006/t20200602_463888.shtml); [accessible copy from the Supreme People's Procuratorate](https://www.spp.gov.cn/zdgz/202006/t20200602_463886.shtml); [France's public-service guide to selling an occupied rental home](https://www.service-public.fr/particuliers/vosdroits/F32001); [Sheffield City Council, tenancy rights when a rented home is sold](https://www.sheffield.gov.uk/housing/homelessness/lose-home/understanding-tenancy-disputes/your-landlord-wants-sell-property); [UK government, England's possession ground for a landlord intending to sell](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/grounds-for-possession-tenant-guidance/grounds-for-possession-guidance-for-tenants).
- Notes: In France, an occupied home can be sold and the lease normally continues on the same terms with the new owner; the rules for a later notice differ by tenancy type. In England, a sale does not itself cancel a tenancy, but a landlord who intends to sell may have a separate legal route to seek possession, with conditions and notice. There is no single US or Europe-wide rule covering every lease: check the law where the home is located. In all cases, keep the signed lease, bank records, messages and evidence of when you took possession.

### 6. Before signing, check the right to rent out the home and any mortgage; label every payment
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: Asking for documents and checking the payee need not cost anything; allow about half an hour. An official property-register search may carry a fee.
- In plain language: Do not pay rent to someone who has not shown a right to let the home. Before signing, ask for evidence of ownership or other authority to rent it out, and check available official records for mortgages and other restrictions. Use a traceable payment method and label the payment with the address, month and purpose, such as “rent for [address], October 2026”. If a dispute arises, the record helps show who received what and why.
- Benefit: Paying an unauthorized person or overlooking a charge on the property can put your money or occupation at risk. A contract and clearly identified transfers make the payment trail easier to prove. The claim that these are the two most common causes of severe loss is not backed by a frequency study; this advice describes practical risks rather than a measured ranking.
- Evidence grade: C
- Sources: Author's experience; no direct study establishing how often these losses occur. [China's Housing Rental Regulation, Article 9](https://xzfg.moj.gov.cn/front/law/detail?LawID=1774) requires a landlord to show identity and proof of a lawful right to let. [HM Land Registry's guide for England and Wales](https://www.gov.uk/get-information-about-property-and-land/search-the-register) explains what the title register contains. For written loans and records of marriage-related transfers, see [items 18 and 24 of chapter 8](08_protect_your_rights_and_property.md).
- Notes: In China, Article 9 also requires the landlord to cooperate with lawful checks. In England and Wales, the title register lists the owner and may show mortgages; an online copy is not an official proof of title, and the register does not answer every question about a tenancy. Elsewhere, ask the relevant land registry or a qualified local adviser what can be checked. If an agent signs for the owner, ask for written authority; if someone sublets, check permission under the main lease and local law. A mortgage alone does not show that letting is prohibited. Keep the same care with payment notes and written promises as in [items 18 and 24 of chapter 8](08_protect_your_rights_and_property.md).

### 7. If a Chinese broker handles the price of a resale home, use the designated bank account for client transaction funds
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: Checking the account details requires no purchase, but insisting on the agreed payment method may take a conversation. The bank or transaction professionals may charge their own fees.
- In plain language: A home purchase can involve hundreds of thousands in local currency. For scale, the median price of new US houses sold in August 2026 was $393,700; the median price of older houses sold in France's Savoie department was €310,400 in the notaries' June 2026 report. These are different markets, not prices to apply to every home. In China, if the parties agree that a brokerage will receive and pass on the purchase funds, pay through its designated bank account for client transaction settlements, never a salesperson's personal account. Verify the account and the conditions for releasing the money before paying.
- Benefit: Article 24 of China's Real Estate Brokerage Measures requires funds collected and disbursed by a brokerage for a transaction to move through the client-settlement deposit account it opens at a bank; the payer and brokerage must sign and seal the transfer. Articles 17–19 require clear prices for brokerage services: two brokerages cooperating on one transaction may not raise the client's commission as though it were two, and loan or property-registration help requires informed consent and a separate contract. A designated account is not a guarantee against every loss, so check when and to whom the funds can be released.
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: [China's Ministry of Housing, Real Estate Brokerage Measures, Order No. 8 of 2011, amended by Order No. 29 of 2016, Articles 17–19 and 24](https://www.mohurd.gov.cn/api-gateway/jpaas-web-server/front/document/download?fileName=%E6%88%BF%E5%9C%B0%E4%BA%A7%E7%BB%8F%E7%BA%AA%E7%AE%A1%E7%90%86%E5%8A%9E%E6%B3%95-%E5%9B%BE%E7%89%87%E7%89%88&fileUrl=YW5UzzlvCwcM%2FNHHX%2FtT6BJ6yM%2FAU1LVtmOVIvNjcurGdLAiZRkjaBvXEKJvAoVSuh3FyuPxDVZ3nOhHkmh3vITkKEfE6jGgmAg%2B%2F2wAFchij7skWenPd%2B9EdyPwGcaS%2FD4BU9T7SuybjHQQMVYNrQ%3D%3D); [China Government Gazette, original 2011 link](http://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2011/content_1918920.htm); [US Census Bureau, new-home sales, August 2026](https://www.census.gov/construction/nrs/current/); [French notaries, Savoie market report, June 2026](https://chambre-interdepartementale-de-savoie.notaires.fr/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/CID_Savoie_juin_2026.pdf); [US Consumer Financial Protection Bureau, closing-payment scams](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/archive/blog/mortgage-closing-scams-how-protect-yourself-and-your-closing-funds/); [France's public-service guide to buying an apartment](https://www.service-public.fr/particuliers/vosdroits/F37190).
- Notes: Chinese brokerages must display their service items and prices; an undisclosed charge is not allowed. Two firms working on one transaction cannot add a second commission to the client's bill. Loan and registration work need their own explanation, consent and contract. The Chinese client-account rule does not automatically apply in the US or Europe. In the US, confirm closing wire instructions with trusted professionals using a number agreed earlier, not a number in a last-minute email. In France, the public-service guide says the full purchase price must reach the notary's account before the authentic sale deed is signed. Use the locally required settlement route, and never send a home's price to an agent's personal account. Label transfers as in [item 6](#6-before-signing-check-the-right-to-rent-out-the-home-and-any-mortgage-label-every-payment).

### 8. Avoid illegally divided rooms and nonresidential spaces; check that a rental is safe to live in
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: Inspecting a home costs nothing. Within the same budget, a safer home might be smaller or farther away.
- In plain language: In China, a kitchen, bathroom, balcony, corridor, underground storage room or garage cannot be rented on its own as living space. A 2010 rule also sets an originally designed room as the smallest unit for renting a home, with local minimum floor-area rules. If an unlawful partition must be removed, a tenant may have to move. Before signing, check windows, exits, partitions and wiring. A landlord also cannot raise the rent unilaterally and arbitrarily during the lease term.
- Benefit: Articles 8 and 9 of China's Commercial Housing Rental Measures require the original room to be the smallest rental unit, a local minimum area per resident, and no arbitrary unilateral rent rise during the contract. Article 7 of the newer Housing Rental Regulation, effective in 2025, separately bans renting the listed nonresidential spaces for living, requires local limits on occupants and floor area, and refers to building and fire-safety standards. Both texts matter: the exact “originally designed room” wording is in the 2010 rule, not Article 7 of the 2025 regulation.
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: [China's Ministry of Housing, Commercial Housing Rental Measures (Order No. 6 of 2010), Articles 8–9, original link](http://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2011/content_1845070.htm); [ministry publication](https://www.mohurd.gov.cn/api-gateway/jpaas-web-server/front/document/download?fileName=%E5%95%86%E5%93%81%E6%88%BF%E5%B1%8B%E7%A7%9F%E8%B5%81%E7%AE%A1%E7%90%86%E5%8A%9E%E6%B3%95-%E5%9B%BE%E7%89%87%E7%89%88&fileUrl=YW5UzzlvCwcM%2FNHHX%2FtT6BJ6yM%2FAU1LVtmOVIvNjcurGdLAiZRkjaBvXEKJvAoVSvWjrY6uUK2XHjQXE9I9I1qm5Q9Y7wjDUutXzLmZ6mQdqzgTrfYwn%2F0jPD%2FunCl%2BWQYjzPEaRI8UfEee9A29fJg%3D%3D); [China's Ministry of Justice, Housing Rental Regulation No. 812 (2025), Articles 7 and 39](https://xzfg.moj.gov.cn/front/law/detail?LawID=1774); [UK government guide to safe rentals and room standards in shared homes in England](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/how-to-rent-a-safe-home/how-to-rent-a-safe-home).
- Notes: A new partition may block an escape route; overcrowding can strain facilities and increase fire risk. Look for a usable window, safe exits and adequate electrical facilities. If an authority orders a noncompliant rental changed, do not assume the deposit and prepaid rent are automatically lost: keep the contract and receipts so you can claim what is owed. In England, licensed houses in multiple occupation have minimum sleeping-room sizes and councils may set stricter standards. In the US, check the state and local housing and fire code. Neither jurisdiction automatically applies China's original-room rule or one universal floor-area figure.
