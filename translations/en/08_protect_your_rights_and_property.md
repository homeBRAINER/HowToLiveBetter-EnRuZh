# 8. Protect your freedom and property

> Partial translation: the introduction and entries 1–12 of 35. Entries 13–35 remain in [Chinese](../../book/08-别把自己搭进去.md). The numbered laws and penalties describe China; regional notes are separate. Clarifications are labeled. New reference checks: September 21, 2026.

This chapter deals with money and personal liberty: legal liability, losses you can avoid, and your procedural rights. It does not convert these outcomes into years of life. Article references point to the official texts used for verification, with qualifications where needed. The order puts potentially costly incidents that are easy to prevent first, measures needing advance preparation in the middle, and experience-based advice last.

### 1. After a road crash, stop, help, and call the police—do not flee
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: No application fee. Stop, switch on your hazard lights, help injured people, and call the police. Allow time for traffic officers to arrive; 1–2 hours is a rough estimate, not a maximum. For a minor crash in China with no injuries and no disagreement about the facts, document the scene and record names, phone numbers, license plates, driver-license numbers, insurance-certificate numbers, and points of impact. Where the self-settlement rules apply, both parties sign the record before clearing the road and discussing compensation.
- In plain language: Fleeing a crash in China normally leaves you responsible for the whole accident unless evidence of the other party's fault reduces that responsibility. A criminal hit-and-run can lead to license revocation and a lifetime ban on obtaining another license. Where the traffic-accident offense applies, fleeing carries 3–7 years in prison; causing a death through flight carries more than 7 years. Commercial insurance can exclude the loss, leaving you with a large bill. Stay, arrange help, and report the crash.
- Benefit: Under Chinese rules, the fleeing party bears full accident responsibility, subject to reduction when the other party's fault is proved. Criminal hit-and-run can bring license revocation and a lifetime driving ban. The traffic-accident offense carries 3–7 years for flight and more than 7 years where flight causes death. The 2020 commercial motor-insurance model terms exclude vehicle-damage and third-party claims involving flight or intentional destruction or falsification of the scene or destruction of evidence. An uninsured compensation bill could reach hundreds of thousands of CNY; this is an illustrative Chinese loss, not a fixed penalty or a measured average.
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: Standing Committee of the National People's Congress (2021). Road Traffic Safety Law, 2021 revision, Articles 70 and 101, reproduced by Beijing traffic police. <https://jtgl.beijing.gov.cn/jgj/jgxx/flfg/fl/205308/index.html>; State Council (2017). Implementing Regulations for the Road Traffic Safety Law, Order No. 405, 2017 revision, Articles 86 and 92. <http://xzfg.moj.gov.cn/front/law/detail?LawID=75>; National People's Congress (1997). Criminal Law, Article 133. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/fl/201802/t20180206_364975.shtml>; Insurance Association of China (2020). Commercial Motor Insurance Model Terms, 2020 edition, Articles 9 and 22. <https://www.iachina.cn/art/2020/9/4/art_24_104621.html>
- Notes: When someone is injured, arrange urgent help and notify the police; if rescuing someone requires moving objects at the scene, mark their positions. Saying you did not notice the impact does not automatically settle whether your departure was flight; if unsure, report it. **Legal clarification:** [Beijing police explain](https://gaj.beijing.gov.cn/xxfb/fjjx/202204/t20220422_2686215.html) the distinction between noncriminal flight and criminal hit-and-run; a lifetime ban is not the automatic consequence of every minor incident. The insurance text is an industry model: check your own policy and applicable law. Compulsory third-party insurance is a separate scheme. The wait can exceed the planning estimate. **US/Europe:** reporting, moving vehicles, exchanging details, and insurance duties depend on the state or country. For injuries or immediate danger, call the local emergency service; follow local police and insurer instructions rather than applying Chinese deadlines or penalties.

### 2. If you have sent money to a scammer, contact the bank and police immediately
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: Reporting the scam does not require paying a recovery intermediary. Give the transfer time, amount, and recipient account; preserve transfer screenshots and messages. In China, report a completed fraud to 110 and contact your bank or payment provider immediately.
- In plain language: Act as soon as you notice the fraud. Chinese police can use emergency payment stops and rapid freezing procedures, and banks and payment providers must cooperate. Recovery becomes harder when money moves through several accounts; do not spend the first minutes investigating or confronting the caller yourself. Report even if more time has passed. The Chinese 96110 line provides fraud warnings and advice; take a warning seriously, but do not treat a displayed number as proof of identity or transfer money on a caller's instructions.
- Benefit: China's Anti-Telecom and Online Fraud Law provides for prompt inquiries into implicated funds, emergency payment stops, and rapid freezing, with cooperation from banks and payment institutions. Frozen funds can be returned through the prescribed process. Earlier reporting can help prevent further transfers; no recovery percentage is established here. The national 96110 line supports warnings and intervention. The law took effect in December 2022.
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: Standing Committee of the National People's Congress (2022). Anti-Telecom and Online Fraud Law, Articles 20 and 34. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/fl/202209/t20220902_575631.shtml>; Fujian Public Security Department (2023). Answer calls from 96110; provincial police guidance. <http://gat.fujian.gov.cn/ztzl/fjjffpzxrx/spjq/202303/t20230306_6126156.htm>
- Notes: The law establishes payment-stop and freezing mechanisms, not a guaranteed refund. Recovery depends in part on whether funds remain available when the report is made. The National Anti-Fraud Center app and 96110 guidance come from Fujian police because the central Ministry of Public Security page was unavailable. Requests to move money to a “safe account” from supposed police, prosecutors, or court staff are scams. **Correction:** [official Guangdong guidance](https://www.gd.gov.cn/gdywdt/bmdt/content/post_2859267.html) distinguishes 96110 warnings/advice from 110 crime reports. There is no established universal deadline of a few dozen minutes after which reporting is pointless. **US/Europe:** contact the bank or payment provider through a trusted channel and ask whether the transfer can be stopped or reversed. The [FTC guide](https://consumer.ftc.gov/articles/what-do-if-you-were-scammed) covers payment-specific steps and US reporting. [Europol lists national cybercrime reporting routes](https://www.europol.europa.eu/report-a-crime/report-cybercrime-online); reports go to the relevant national or local authority, not directly to Europol. Refund rights depend on the payment and jurisdiction.

### 3. Do not trust an unexpected caller: protect your details and verify every payment request
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No purchase is needed. Remember four habits: question unexpected calls, keep sensitive information private, avoid unsolicited links, and verify transfers. If someone asks you to transfer money, share your screen, install an unfamiliar app, open a link, or read out a verification code, end the call and contact the organization through an independently checked official number. In China, the National Anti-Fraud Center app is another official prevention tool.
- In plain language: Hang up before acting on a financial request, then verify it yourself. Scammers pose as police, prosecutors, court staff, customer support, managers, or a platform's fraud team. Their stories include “cooperate with an investigation,” “cancel a membership,” paid fake-order tasks, or inside investment information. Recognizing the setup before paying costs less than trying to recover the money afterward.
- Benefit: China's law requires public education about telecom and online fraud and warning systems involving police, finance, telecom, and internet authorities. People identified as potential victims should receive timely warnings. The national 96110 line is used for this intervention; do not dismiss a genuine warning, and independently verify any accompanying request. Prevention avoids the need to seek recovery, whose outcome depends partly on whether money is still available when reported; see entry 2. The national law has applied since December 2022.
- Evidence grade: B
- Sources: Standing Committee of the National People's Congress (2022). Anti-Telecom and Online Fraud Law, Articles 8, 20, and 34. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/fl/202209/t20220902_575631.shtml>; Fujian Public Security Department (2023). Answer calls from 96110; includes the four-habit prevention slogan and a warning about scam calls beginning with 192. <http://gat.fujian.gov.cn/ztzl/fjjffpzxrx/spjq/202303/t20230306_6126156.htm>
- Notes: Grade B reflects official prevention guidance, not a measured reduction in fraud among people who memorize it. The Ministry of Public Security website, mps.gov.cn, was unavailable during the source check; the provincial reproduction is used. Impersonated officials, support teams, managers, and fraud teams use different stories to push the same unsafe actions. **Clarification:** a phone prefix, caller ID, or an official-looking app is not identity proof; a 192 prefix alone does not establish fraud. Outside China, use your bank's and national authorities' official channels; the Chinese app and 96110 are not foreign reporting services.

### 4. A familiar face or voice is not identity proof; hang up and call a number you already trust
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: One extra call and a few minutes. Limit public close-up face videos and long voice recordings that could be copied.
- In plain language: A convincing face or voice can be faked. Chinese police guidance suggests asking for a continuous head turn or for a hand to cover the whole face and move away quickly; artifacts may appear, but passing that test proves nothing. The stronger check is to hang up and call the person's old number already saved in your contacts, or confirm through someone you both know. If the caller refuses and says the signal is bad or they are in a meeting, do not send money until you have independent confirmation. AI-content labels also cannot authenticate the caller: a scammer may omit them.
- Benefit: The Ministry of Public Security's cyber police describe three checks: movement, voice details, and independent contact. Head turns or brief full-face occlusion may expose stuttering, blur, or distortion; unusual breathing pauses, emotional inflection, mechanical sound, or background noise may also raise suspicion. The central step is to call a familiar number independently—not the suspicious caller's number—or ask a trusted mutual contact. The practical rule is to question video and voice and verify through another channel. Chinese deep-synthesis rules prohibit fraudulent use and require prominent labels for covered face-generation, face-replacement, synthetic-voice, and voice-imitation services. The 2025 labeling rules prohibit malicious removal, alteration, forgery, or concealment of labels; absence of a label does not prove authenticity. In one reported investigation, police uncovered a 14-person group, more than 50,000 synthesized moving-face videos, more than 500,000 personal-information records, and seized 60 cloud servers. The 2025 labeling measures took effect on September 1, 2025.
- Evidence grade: B
- Sources: Ministry of Public Security Cyber Security Bureau (2025). Seeing is not believing: beware new AI scams, reproduced by Fujian police. <http://gat.fujian.gov.cn/ztzl/fjjffpzxrx/spjq/202509/t20250922_7013008.htm>; Cyberspace Administration of China, Ministry of Industry and Information Technology, and Ministry of Public Security (2022). Deep Synthesis Internet Information Service Provisions, Articles 6, 16, and 17; effective January 10, 2023. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/2022-12/12/content_5731431.htm>; Cyberspace Administration of China and three other authorities (2025). Measures for Labeling AI-Generated and Synthetic Content, Article 10; effective September 1, 2025. <https://www.cac.gov.cn/2025-03/14/c_1743654684782215.htm>; Ministry of Public Security Criminal Investigation Bureau and CCTV News (2026). Police uncover more than 50,000 synthesized moving-face videos: who is copying your face?, reproduced by Fujian police. <http://gat.fujian.gov.cn/ztzl/fjjffpzxrx/spjq/202603/t20260330_7117146.htm>
- Notes: Grade B reflects official advice and a case report, not a measured reduction in fraud from these checks. Use the old number in your own contacts, not caller ID or a new number sent in chat. Refusal to perform a movement or join a video call, including excuses about a meeting or poor signal, is a reason to stop the transaction and verify independently; it is not proof of a crime. **Technical clarification:** movement and voice artifacts are clues, not reliable authentication tests. Models change, and even independent contact must use a channel you actually trust. Chinese deep-synthesis duties began under the 2023 rules; September 2025 is the start of the additional labeling measures, not the first labeling obligation. For money already sent, see entry 2; for protecting face and voice data, see chapter 14, currently in Chinese. The central ministry website returned 521 during the earlier check, so the cyber and criminal-investigation guidance is cited through Fujian's official reproductions. These Chinese labeling laws do not establish US or European duties.

### 5. If you are accused or summoned for questioning, get a lawyer and keep your records
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=多 时间=中 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: Ask about the fee before hiring counsel. Local examples checked September 21, 2026: the [New York City Bar referral service](https://portal.311.nyc.gov/article/?kanumber=KA-01040) offers an initial consultation of up to 30 minutes for 35 USD or free, depending on the case. In Germany, the default fee ceiling for a consumer's first consultation without a separate fee agreement is 190 EUR, plus VAT and necessary expenses ([German Federal Bar](https://www.brak.de/service/verbraucherinformationen/was-kostet-das/)). These are consultation figures, not the cost of defending a whole case. If you cannot afford a lawyer, ask a legal-aid provider about eligibility. During questioning in China, you may refuse questions unrelated to the case; get legal advice about the questions and your procedural position.
- In plain language: In a Chinese criminal investigation, you can appoint a lawyer from your first interrogation or when a compulsory measure is imposed. A detention center generally must arrange a properly requested lawyer visit within 48 hours, and the conversation must not be monitored; certain national-security and terrorism investigations require permission first. No one may force you to incriminate yourself. You may refuse unrelated questions, but Chinese law also requires truthful answers to questions about the case. A summons or compulsory appearance normally lasts no more than 12 hours; the 24-hour exception requires both an especially serious, complex case and a need for detention or arrest. Repeated summonses must not be used to disguise detention. Avoid private contact with the complainant or their family, and discuss any settlement with your lawyer: what you say or agree to could be interpreted as an admission. Preserve messages, call records, and travel records that may support your account.
- Benefit: In China, the right to appoint defense counsel begins with the first interrogation or a compulsory measure; during the investigation, only a lawyer may act as appointed defense counsel. The ordinary limit for arranging a lawyer visit is 48 hours after a request with the required documents, and visits must not be monitored. The permission exception for national-security and terrorism cases still applies. Compelled self-incrimination is prohibited, and questions unrelated to the case may be refused. A summons or compulsory appearance is limited to 12 hours, or 24 hours in the serious, complex cases requiring detention or arrest described above; repeated summonses cannot substitute for detention. Fabricating accusations to have someone criminally prosecuted, in serious circumstances, carries up to 3 years in prison, criminal detention, or noncustodial public supervision; serious consequences carry 3–10 years. State compensation may be available after detention or arrest followed by withdrawal of the case, a decision not to prosecute, or acquittal, provided the statutory conditions are met. Compensation for loss of liberty is calculated per day using the previous year's national average daily wage for employees.
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: Standing Committee of the National People's Congress (2018). Criminal Procedure Law, 2018 amendment, Articles 34, 35, 39, 52, 119, and 120. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/zdgz/201810/t20181027_396818.shtml>; National People's Congress (1997). Criminal Law, Article 243. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/fl/201802/t20180206_364975.shtml>; Standing Committee of the National People's Congress (2012). State Compensation Law, 2012 amendment, Articles 17 and 33, reproduced by the National Bureau of Statistics. <https://www.stats.gov.cn/gk/tjfg/xgfxfg/202503/t20250306_1958899.html>
- Notes: Keep messages, call records, and travel records intact; deleting them can destroy evidence in your favor. Do not approach the other party or their family to negotiate privately. A settlement is not automatically a confession, but legal advice helps you avoid damaging statements and unlawful contact. The false-accusation offense does not cover an honest mistake or an inaccurate report without the required intent. These protections apply regardless of the accused person's gender. Entries 31, 32, and 33 of [this chapter in Chinese](../../book/08-别把自己搭进去.md) cover fabricated accusations, acquittal for insufficient evidence and review proceedings, and daily compensation after wrongful detention. **Legal clarifications:** the [2018 Criminal Procedure Law reproduced by Shenzhen police](https://www.szns.gov.cn/nsnsgafj/gkmlpt/content/11/11403/mpost_11403806.html) sets the visit exception, the additional condition for 24 hours, and the duty to answer case-related questions truthfully; it does not create a general right to have counsel answer every question for you. Under the [Supreme People's Court's compensation filing rules](https://www.court.gov.cn/zixun/xiangqing/3663.html), an initial detention and a formal arrest have different compensation conditions: for detention, illegality or exceeding the lawful time limit matters. Release or acquittal alone does not guarantee compensation; statutory exclusions also apply. **US/Europe:** rights during questioning, appointed counsel, and compensation depend on the jurisdiction and procedural status. Use a local criminal-defense lawyer; [chapter 7, entry 3](07_when_money_is_tight.md) lists legal-aid starting points.

### 6. If you have been drinking, do not drive—even an electric vehicle or “just to move the car”
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: Arrange a sober driver before a gathering, or pay for a taxi or a service that drives your car home. Taxi illustrations checked September 21, 2026: in New York City, a 3-mile metered ride at normal speed works out to about 15 USD before time-of-day or Manhattan surcharges, tolls, waiting, and tip (3 USD start + 15 distance units at 0.70 USD + 0.50 USD MTA charge + 1 USD improvement charge; [TLC rates](https://home4.nyc.gov/site/tlc/passengers/taxi-fare.page)). In Berlin, 5 km works out to about 17.90 EUR before waiting and other applicable extras (4.30 + 3 × 2.80 + 2 × 2.60; [city rates](https://www.berlin.de/en/public-transportation/1756978-2913840-taxi-phone-numbers-fares-rules.en.html)). These are calculations for specified trips, not live quotes or designated-driver prices. Check the actual fare before booking.
- In plain language: In China, driving a motor vehicle after drinking can mean a 6-month license suspension and a 1,000–2,000 CNY fine. A repeat offense after a previous penalty can bring administrative detention and license revocation. Driving while legally intoxicated can bring revocation, a 5-year ban on getting another license, and criminal liability. A conviction can affect official background checks and some occupations. Rules for commercial transport vehicles are stricter: intoxicated driving brings a 10-year license ban and a permanent ban on driving those vehicles. Commercial motor policies can exclude losses involving alcohol or drugs. Moving a car only a short distance is still driving, although criminal liability depends on the legal conditions and circumstances; see the clarification below.
- Benefit: Chinese motor-vehicle rules provide for a 6-month suspension and a 1,000–2,000 CNY fine for driving after drinking. Repeating it after a previous penalty brings up to 10 days of administrative detention, another 1,000–2,000 CNY fine, and license revocation. Driving while legally intoxicated brings revocation and a 5-year ban on obtaining a new license; where the dangerous-driving offense is established, the penalty is criminal detention plus a fine. Driving a commercial transport vehicle after drinking brings 15 days of administrative detention, a 5,000 CNY fine, revocation, and a 5-year ban; intoxicated driving brings a 10-year ban and permanent exclusion from commercial transport driving, as well as criminal liability where the offense is established. An alcohol-related major crash that constitutes a crime brings a lifetime driving ban. The 2020 commercial motor-insurance model terms exclude vehicle-damage and third-party claims involving driving after alcohol or illegal drugs.
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: Standing Committee of the National People's Congress (2021). Road Traffic Safety Law, 2021 revision, Article 91, reproduced by Beijing traffic police. <https://jtgl.beijing.gov.cn/jgj/jgxx/flfg/fl/205308/index.html>; Standing Committee of the National People's Congress (2015). Criminal Law Amendment IX, Article 8, inserting the relevant provisions of Article 133-1. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/fl/201802/t20180205_364562.shtml>; Insurance Association of China (2020). Commercial Motor Insurance Model Terms, 2020 edition, Articles 9 and 22. <https://www.iachina.cn/art/2020/9/4/art_24_104621.html>
- Notes: A criminal conviction for intoxicated driving can affect background checks and eligibility for some occupations. The legal distinction between driving after drinking and driving while intoxicated uses blood-alcohol thresholds; the full applicable national standard and its numerical boundaries have not been checked for this entry. Do not estimate your legal status from how sober you feel. **Legal clarification:** the [2023 joint guidance on intoxicated driving](https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/xwfbh/wsfbt/202312/t20231218_637161.shtml) requires the legal elements of a motor vehicle, a road, and intoxication. An electric car or electric motorcycle is not legally interchangeable with every electric bicycle. The guidance allows some negligible cases, including qualifying short vehicle moves without aggravating circumstances, to be treated as noncriminal; minor cases can also receive other lenient dispositions. “Just moving the car” is therefore neither a universal exemption nor automatic proof of a criminal conviction. The insurance wording is an industry model, not every policy or the separate compulsory-insurance scheme. **US/Europe:** alcohol limits, vehicle classifications, penalties, and insurance rules vary by state or country. The practical decision is to arrange a sober ride, not to apply Chinese thresholds or penalties abroad.

### 7. Do not lend your bank card, SIM, or payment account for someone else's transfers: money-mule work is not a safe side job
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: No purchase or fee. Refuse offers to pay you for letting money pass through your account or for handing over a card or SIM.
- In plain language: In China, unlawfully selling, renting out, or lending bank accounts, phone cards, payment accounts, or internet accounts is itself punishable. Illegal proceeds can be confiscated, with a fine of 1–10 times those proceeds. If there are no proceeds or they are below 20,000 CNY, the fine can instead be up to 200,000 CNY. Serious cases can also bring up to 15 days of administrative detention. Other measures may include credit-record entries and account restrictions. Knowingly helping online crime through payment processing, in serious circumstances, can lead to up to 3 years in prison. Investigators can consider circumstantial evidence of knowledge, including a fee you received or an implausible transaction volume; those facts must be assessed in context.
- Benefit: China's Anti-Telecom and Online Fraud Law prohibits unlawful sale, rental, or lending of phone cards, bank accounts, payment accounts, and internet accounts. It provides for confiscation of illegal proceeds and fines of 1–10 times those proceeds; where proceeds are absent or below 20,000 CNY, the maximum fine is 200,000 CNY. Serious cases can also bring up to 15 days of administrative detention. Additional measures may include credit-record entries, limits on account functions, and suspension of transactions that do not take place at a bank counter, under the applicable conditions. Knowingly providing payment-settlement or other assistance for online crime, in serious circumstances, is a separate criminal offense punishable by up to 3 years in prison or criminal detention, with a fine, or a fine alone.
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: Standing Committee of the National People's Congress (2022). Anti-Telecom and Online Fraud Law, Articles 31 and 44. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/fl/202209/t20220902_575631.shtml>; Standing Committee of the National People's Congress (2015). Criminal Law Amendment IX, Article 29, inserting Article 287-2. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/fl/201802/t20180205_364562.shtml>
- Notes: Receiving a fee and handling an obviously abnormal volume of transfers can support a finding that you knew what was happening; neither is an automatic substitute for examining the evidence. If your card receives criminal proceeds, it may be frozen and other restrictions may follow even without a criminal conviction. **Legal clarification:** [Articles 31 and 44](https://www.samr.gov.cn/zw/zfxxgk/fdzdgknr/bgt/art/2023/art_4d2f14016988480e979f4cd49251e07a.html) concern unlawful transfers or use of accounts; they should not be read as banning every properly authorized family or business arrangement. The alternative fine ceiling also covers proceeds below 20,000 CNY, not only cases with no proceeds. Whether criminal knowledge and serious circumstances are established is a separate question. **US/Europe:** the Chinese offenses and fine amounts do not carry over to other jurisdictions. If someone offers a job that requires receiving and forwarding strangers' money through your personal account, decline it and contact your bank if transfers have already occurred.

### 8. Check your credit report for loans or cards you did not open; China provides two free reports a year
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No fee within the applicable free-report entitlement. Allow about 10 minutes for a straightforward online request; registration, identity checks, and delivery can take longer. Free-report rules for the US and Europe are described below.
- In plain language: In China, you are entitled to two free copies of your credit report each year. Checking can reveal loans or cards opened with stolen identity details before a rejected mortgage application exposes a problem that has been building for years. Negative information is generally retained for 5 years after the underlying adverse conduct or event ends, then must be deleted. If you dispute an error, the credit bureau or information provider must investigate, deal with it, and send a written response within 20 days. Use the People's Bank of China's official Credit Reference Center or an authorized bank channel. Avoid unfamiliar “credit-check” apps that ask for sensitive data.
- Benefit: China's Credit Reporting Industry Regulations give individuals two free personal credit reports annually. Negative information is retained for 5 years from the end of the adverse conduct or event and must be deleted after those 5 years. If information is wrong or missing, you can challenge it. The credit bureau or information provider must mark it as disputed, investigate and handle the dispute, and respond in writing within 20 days of receiving it.
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: State Council (2013). Credit Reporting Industry Regulations, Order No. 631, Articles 16, 17, and 25. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/2013-01/29/content_2303.htm>
- Notes: Identity misuse can remain unnoticed for years, until you apply for a mortgage or another loan. In China, go through the official Credit Reference Center or an authorized mobile-banking service. Unfamiliar third-party apps can collect the very information you are trying to protect; not every third-party service is fraudulent, but check who operates it and what authorization it has. **Clarification:** the [regulations](https://www.samr.gov.cn/zw/zfxxgk/fdzdgknr/bgt/art/2023/art_f06d5be45fcf464abe3fbdb9b2b3b37c.html) start the 5-year clock when the adverse conduct or event ends, not simply when a loan was opened. The 20-day deadline is for investigation and a written answer, not a guarantee that any disputed debt will be erased. **US:** the [FTC](https://consumer.ftc.gov/articles/free-credit-reports) directs consumers to [AnnualCreditReport.com](https://www.annualcreditreport.com/) for free reports from Equifax, Experian, and TransUnion. Each bureau currently offers a free weekly report through that site; this is more frequent than the statutory annual entitlement. **EU:** the [European Commission](https://commission.europa.eu/law/law-topic/data-protection/information-business-and-organisations/dealing-requests-individuals_en) explains the right to a free copy of personal data held by an organization. This can help you inspect data held by a credit bureau, but it is not a uniform Europe-wide entitlement to two commercial credit reports per year. Excessive or manifestly unfounded requests can have different rules; check the bureau and your country's procedure. Regional information checked September 21, 2026.

### 9. If a confrontation escalates, get to safety and call the police; do not start a fight
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: No purchase needed. Resist insults and shoving, move toward other people if you can do so safely, and call the police. Nearby security cameras may help preserve evidence.
- In plain language: In China, assault ordinarily brings 5–10 days of administrative detention and a 500–1,000 CNY fine. Group assaults, attacks on certain vulnerable people, and repeated or multiple-victim assaults carry 10–15 days and a 1,000–2,000 CNY fine. Intentional injury that reaches the criminal threshold can bring up to 3 years in prison; serious injury carries 3–10 years. Joining a fight can make a self-defense claim much harder. Move to safety, call the police, and preserve footage rather than escalating. You may defend yourself against an ongoing unlawful attack, but once the danger has actually ended, chasing and hitting someone is retaliation. Who struck first is not the sole legal test.
- Benefit: Under Chinese law, the ordinary administrative penalty for assault or intentional injury is 5–10 days of detention plus 500–1,000 CNY. The aggravated range is 10–15 days plus 1,000–2,000 CNY for a group assault; an assault on a disabled person, a pregnant person, someone under 14, or someone aged 70 or older; repeated assaults; or one assault against multiple people. Group brawling that falls under the public-order provision can bring up to 15 days of detention, with an additional fine of up to 2,000 CNY. Criminal intentional injury ordinarily carries up to 3 years in prison, criminal detention, or noncustodial public supervision; serious injury carries 3–10 years. A mutual fight is distinguished from self-defense by its circumstances and intent. A response can generally qualify as defense if the other person initiates clearly excessive violence, or continues attacking while you try to avoid the conflict. Excessive defense requires both clearly exceeding what is necessary and causing serious harm; even then, punishment must be reduced or waived. These are nationwide Chinese rules; the revised Public Security Administration Punishments Law took effect on January 1, 2026.

### 11. Do not attack people who have nothing to do with your grievance; endangering the public can bring a long prison term, life imprisonment, or death under Chinese law
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: No financial outlay.
- In plain language: Deliberately driving into a crowd or otherwise putting the public in danger can constitute a serious crime in China even if nobody is injured. The ordinary statutory range without serious consequences is 3–10 years in prison; serious injury, death, or major property damage brings at least 10 years, life imprisonment, or a death sentence. In Zhuhai, Fan Weiqiu was dissatisfied with the division of property after divorce. He drove into people exercising at a sports center, killing 35 and injuring 43, and was later executed. Those people had nothing to do with his divorce. Saying that you do not care whether you live is not, by itself, a reason for a lighter sentence. Even a threat of arson or an explosion that disrupts public order can bring 5–10 days of administrative detention and a possible fine.
- Benefit: Article 114 of China's Criminal Law covers arson, flooding, explosions, releasing toxic, radioactive, or infectious substances, and other comparably dangerous methods that endanger public safety. Without serious consequences, the statutory range is 3–10 years in prison: an injury is not required for this offense. Under Article 115, serious injury, death, or major loss of public or private property carries at least 10 years, life imprisonment, or death. In the official case account, Fan Weiqiu, distressed by the breakdown of his marriage, unhappy with his life, and dissatisfied with the divorce property settlement, decided to vent his grievance by driving into a crowd. On the evening of November 11, 2024, he drove into people exercising at Zhuhai Sports Center, killing 35 and injuring 43. The first-instance court sentenced him to death for endangering public safety by dangerous means and deprived him of political rights for life. He was executed on January 20, 2025 after Supreme People's Court approval. His victims were people exercising, unrelated to his property dispute. These are Chinese offenses, penalties, and case facts.

### 12. If you think about dying and taking someone with you, treat it as an emergency: step away from danger and call for help
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: Moving away from dangerous items costs nothing. US 988 calls and texts are free (0 USD); Germany's TelefonSeelsorge is free (0 EUR). The hard step can be admitting that you need support now and letting someone you trust arrange safe storage of car keys and dangerous items.
- In plain language: An acute crisis can move quickly. In one study of 82 people who survived a suicide attempt, 47.6% reported no more than 10 minutes between the first persistent suicidal thoughts in that episode and the attempt. Put distance between yourself and the car, knives, gas controls, ropes, and other things you could use to hurt yourself or someone else. Do not carry a weapon over to another person; step away and ask for help securing it safely. You do not have to solve the whole problem before asking for help. Restrictions on painkiller availability were associated with 43% fewer related suicide deaths, and barriers at frequently used jumping sites with an 86% reduction there. These findings support creating distance from danger, not a promise that a crisis ends after ten minutes. In the US, call or text **988**; in Germany, call **116 123**. If someone may be hurt now, call emergency services—**911** in the US or **112** in the EU. In China, the psychological-help number is **12356**. Do not drive or keep working in a role where others' safety depends on you while you cannot do so safely; arrange a handover and support.
- Benefit: In interviews with 82 suicide-attempt survivors within 3 days of the attempt, 47.6% reported a period of 10 minutes or less from the first persistent suicidal thoughts in the current episode to the attempt. A 10-year systematic review found growing evidence for restricting access to lethal means: painkiller restrictions were associated with a 43% decrease in related suicide deaths, and barriers at jumping hotspots with an 86% decrease there (95% confidence interval 79%–91%). Creating distance from the steering wheel, knives, gas controls, and ropes can provide time to get help; ten minutes is not a proven safety threshold. China's nationwide psychological-help number is 12356. The national notice required connections by May 1, 2025 and at least 18 hours of service daily; it did not promise that every local line would operate around the clock.
