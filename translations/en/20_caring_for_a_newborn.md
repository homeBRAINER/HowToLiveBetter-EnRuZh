# 20. Caring for a newborn

This chapter tracks infant mortality and money. It covers only a few measures with substantial effects and strong evidence; day-to-day childcare details are beyond this book's scope. For things to arrange before birth and on the day you leave the hospital, see [chapter 27](../../book/27-怀孕和生产.md), currently available in Chinese.

The introduction and all 11 entries are translated below.

### 1. Put your baby to sleep on their back, on a firm, flat surface, in a separate crib or bassinet in your room
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=是 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: No extra cost if you already have a suitable sleep space. If you need a mattress, examples observed on 25 September 2026 were about $100 for a US full-size crib mattress or €50 for a 60 × 120 cm mattress in Germany; neither price includes the crib.
- In plain language: Put your baby on their back for every sleep. Use a firm, flat, non-inclined surface in your room, but give the baby their own sleep space. Keep pillows, blankets, bumpers, stuffed toys and head-shaping pillows out of it. An inclined bassinet or infant seat is not a regular place to sleep. About 3,500 infants a year have died from sleep-related causes in the United States; safe-sleep practices help reduce the risk.
- Benefit: About 3,500 US infants a year have died from sleep-related causes: sudden infant death syndrome, deaths with an unknown cause and accidental suffocation in bed. The American Academy of Pediatrics recommends placing babies on their backs on a firm, non-inclined sleep surface, sharing a room but not a bed, and avoiding soft bedding and overheating. Other risk-reduction measures include breastfeeding, avoiding nicotine and alcohol exposure, keeping up with routine vaccinations and offering a pacifier.
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: Moon RY, Carlin RF, Hand I, et al. (2022). Sleep-Related Infant Deaths: Updated 2022 Recommendations for Reducing Infant Deaths in the Sleep Environment. Pediatrics, 150(1), e2022057990. <https://doi.org/10.1542/peds.2022-057990>; American Academy of Pediatrics, safe-sleep guidance. <https://www.healthychildren.org/English/ages-stages/baby/sleep/Pages/a-parents-guide-to-safe-sleep.aspx>; CDC, US sleep-related infant death figures. <https://www.cdc.gov/mmwr/volumes/67/wr/mm6701e1.htm>. Mattress price examples, 25 September 2026: United States — <https://www.target.com/p/-/A-1013971755>; Germany — <https://www.ikea.com/de/de/p/krummelur-schaummatratze-babybett-20593406/>.
- Notes: Keeping soft objects out includes nursing pillows, even if they look safe. Do not use an inclined bassinet or infant seat as a routine sleep space. If the baby falls asleep in a seat, move them to an appropriate sleep surface as soon as practical. This rule may be hard to maintain when older relatives advise otherwise. The 3,500 figure describes the US burden of sleep-related deaths, not deaths prevented by this single measure. A listed mattress price is not proof that a product is safe: check firmness, a flat surface and a snug fit in the crib.

### 2. Check the hepatitis B birth dose before discharge; WHO recommends it within 24 hours
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: The vaccine in China's national immunization program is free. No equipment purchase ($0/€0); any charge for clinical care in the United States or Europe depends on the country and coverage. Ask the maternity team about your baby's entitlement and schedule.
- In plain language: Check the hepatitis B vaccination plan before your baby leaves the hospital. WHO recommends the first dose as soon as possible after birth, within 24 hours. Infection acquired in infancy or early childhood becomes chronic in about 95% of cases, compared with less than 5% for infections acquired in adulthood. If the mother tests positive for hepatitis B surface antigen (HBsAg), tell the birth team: the baby also needs hepatitis B immune globulin (HBIG) promptly after birth.
- Benefit: WHO recommends that all infants receive the hepatitis B vaccine as soon as possible after birth, within 24 hours. Infection acquired in infancy or early childhood becomes chronic in about 95% of cases; infection acquired in adulthood does so in less than 5%. That difference makes early protection particularly valuable.
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: World Health Organization. Hepatitis B fact sheet, 28 July 2026. <https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/hepatitis-b>; US CDC. Hepatitis B birth-dose policy, 16 December 2025. <https://cdc.gov/media/releases/2025/fact-sheet-hepatitis-b-immunization.html>; US CDC. Clinical Overview of Perinatal Hepatitis B. <https://www.cdc.gov/hepatitis-b/hcp/perinatal-provider-overview/>; European Centre for Disease Prevention and Control. Hepatitis B vaccination policies in EU/EEA countries. <https://www.ecdc.europa.eu/en/publications-data/hepatitis-b-vaccination-policies-practices-and-challenges-achieving-hepatitis>.
- Notes: Have the mother's HBsAg result available before delivery. In US guidance, a baby born to an HBsAg-positive mother receives both the vaccine and HBIG within 12 hours, at separate injection sites. **United States:** since December 2025, when the mother tests negative, the CDC recommends an individual decision with a clinician about giving the birth dose or starting the series later; a positive or unknown maternal status calls for prompt protection under the CDC pathway. **Europe:** national schedules differ; follow the maternity team's schedule, especially if the mother has hepatitis B. Maternal testing is also discussed in [chapter 1](01_avoid_dying_early.md).

### 3. Follow your child's vaccination schedule and catch up on missed doses
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=中 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: Vaccines in China's national immunization program cost the family nothing: the law bars vaccination clinics from charging for them. In the United States, an eligible child can receive a Vaccines for Children (VFC) vaccine for $0, though a provider may charge an administration or other visit fee; the child cannot be refused a VFC vaccine because the family cannot pay the administration fee. In Germany, statutory insurance covers recommended childhood vaccinations, so the covered vaccine itself costs the insured family €0. Entitlement and charges elsewhere in Europe vary. Expect several visits as your child grows, and keep their vaccination record.
- In plain language: Check your country's current schedule and track each dose your child receives. If an appointment is missed, ask the vaccination clinic for a catch-up plan. China's program for children under 18 and the US CDC's catch-up guidance say a delayed multidose series does not need to start over: give the missing doses. China permits the same type of vaccine from another manufacturer when the original product is unavailable; elsewhere, check the interchangeability rules for that specific vaccine. Stable eczema, hives, allergic rhinitis or asthma, and ordinary newborn jaundice are not by themselves reasons to put off every vaccination.
- Benefit: In China, nurseries and schools must check children's vaccination records on enrollment, report missing national-program doses to a vaccination clinic, and help prompt parents to catch up. The 2026 Chinese schedule includes human papillomavirus (HPV) vaccination in the national program. It moved the first diphtheria, tetanus and pertussis (DTaP) dose from 3 months to 2 months, with 5 doses: one each at 2, 4, 6 and 18 months and at 6 years. Since March 2026, eligible children in Tibet, Qinghai, Xinjiang and the Xinjiang Production and Construction Corps have also received routine Japanese encephalitis vaccination. National-program vaccines may be given at the same visit under the routine or catch-up schedule: injections go into different sites and are never mixed in one syringe. If two injectable live attenuated vaccines are not given on the same day, separate them by at least 28 days. China's program does not require an interval between its inactivated or oral live attenuated vaccines and other vaccines when they are given separately. For children under 18, catch up on missing doses as soon as possible without restarting the series; if necessary, continue with the same vaccine type from a different manufacturer. These dates and enrollment rules describe China, not a US or Europe-wide schedule.
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: Standing Committee of the National People's Congress (2019). Vaccine Administration Law of China, articles 48–49. <http://ynswsjkw.yn.gov.cn/html/2019/faguiguizhangxin_0731/6509.html>; government publication of the law. <https://www.samr.gov.cn/zw/zfxxgk/fdzdgknr/fgs/art/2023/art_dde03480953841a5912c864dc29003d0.html>. China's National Disease Control and Prevention Administration and National Health Commission (2026). Q&A on the 2026 national childhood immunization schedule. <https://www.ndcpa.gov.cn/jbkzzx/c100013/common/content/content_2073002930292035584.html>; reprint by the Liaoning Provincial Health Commission. <https://wsjk.ln.gov.cn/wsjk/index/syxwxx/zcjd/2026080900053928035/index.shtml>. US CDC: child and adolescent catch-up schedule, vaccination contraindications, and VFC costs. <https://www.cdc.gov/vaccines/hcp/imz-schedules/child-adolescent-catch-up.html>; <https://www.cdc.gov/vaccines/hcp/imz-best-practices/contraindications-precautions.html>; <https://www.cdc.gov/vaccines-for-children/vfc-information-for-parents/index.html>. European Centre for Disease Prevention and Control: national vaccine scheduler. <https://vaccine-schedule.ecdc.europa.eu/Scheduler/ByCountry>. German Federal Ministry of Health: coverage of recommended vaccinations. <https://www.bundesgesundheitsministerium.de/themen/praevention/impfungen/schutzimpfungen/>.
- Notes: China's 2026 guidance says a family history of allergies, food or medication allergies, or an unspecific label of “allergic” is not itself a contraindication. A child whose eczema, hives, allergic rhinitis or asthma is stable may be vaccinated even while taking oral allergy medicine or inhaled corticosteroids; physiological or breast-milk jaundice in a newborn is not itself a reason to defer. A previous acute severe allergic reaction to the same vaccine, or a severe allergy to one of its components, is a contraindication to the relevant vaccine. Immunodeficiency, chemotherapy, radiotherapy and immunosuppressive treatment require an individual assessment of the child's condition and vaccine type; some doses must wait during intensive treatment. If jaundice accompanies a serious illness requiring hospital care, vaccination may also wait until the child is stable. See [entry 2](#2-check-the-hepatitis-b-birth-dose-before-discharge-who-recommends-it-within-24-hours) for the hepatitis B birth dose. In China, vaccines outside the national program may carry both a vaccine charge and an administration fee. In the US and Europe, use the local schedule and payment rules; Chinese school checks and vaccine dates do not transfer across borders.

### 4. Breastfeed exclusively for the first 6 months, then add foods and continue breastfeeding
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=多 毅力=是 收益=中 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: If breast milk meets your baby's needs, buying formula costs $0 in the United States or €0 in Europe. That avoids formula purchases; it does not mean feeding takes no resources. Breastfeeding takes time, and support or supplies may have a cost when needed.
- In plain language: If possible, begin breastfeeding within the first hour after birth. For the first 6 months, give only breast milk, not even water, unless a clinician advises a medical exception. At about 6 months, add safe, nutritious foods and continue breastfeeding to age 2 years or beyond if that works for you and your child. Make sure your baby gets enough iron. China's infant-feeding guide calls for iron-rich animal foods in complementary meals every day. If breast milk is insufficient or breastfeeding is not possible, use an appropriate infant formula and discuss feeding with your baby's clinician.
- Benefit: WHO and UNICEF recommend starting breastfeeding within 1 hour of birth, exclusive breastfeeding for the first 6 months — no other foods or liquids, including water — and safe, adequate complementary foods from 6 months while breastfeeding continues to age 2 years or beyond. China's infant nutrition assessment guide likewise recommends exclusive breastfeeding at ages 0–6 months and, from 6 months, paying close attention to iron: daily complementary foods should include iron-rich animal-source foods. Complementary foods help meet a baby's growing nutrient needs.
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: World Health Organization. Breastfeeding. <https://www.who.int/health-topics/breastfeeding>; WHO, definition of exclusive breastfeeding and medical exceptions. <https://www.who.int/tools/elena/interventions/exclusive-breastfeeding>. China's National Health Commission General Office (2024). Trial Guide for Infant and Young Child Nutrition and Feeding Assessment, document No. 452 of 2024. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202502/content_7002872.htm>; commission publication. <https://www.nhc.gov.cn/fys/c100078/202502/19903ff647694f3a85ed6fe332380b34.shtml>. US CDC. Iron for infants and toddlers. <https://www.cdc.gov/infant-toddler-nutrition/vitamins-minerals/iron.html>.
- Notes: If you cannot breastfeed or do not have enough breast milk, infant formula is an appropriate way to feed your baby; there is no reason to feel guilty. China's guide suggests 1–2 complementary meals a day at 6–8 months and 2–3 at 9–12 months, with at least 4 of 7 food groups each day. It specifies an iron-rich animal-source food, a vegetable, and grains or starchy foods. The US CDC also lists meat, fish, eggs, iron-fortified infant cereal and plant foods as iron sources; if your child's diet excludes whole food groups, discuss their iron needs with a clinician. Exclusive breastfeeding can still include medically indicated oral rehydration solution or drops or syrups of medicines, vitamins or minerals. The cited sources do not support a numerical comparison between breastfeeding and [safe sleep](#1-put-your-baby-to-sleep-on-their-back-on-a-firm-flat-surface-in-a-separate-crib-or-bassinet-in-your-room); both measures matter.

### 5. Mix ordinary powdered formula with water at least 70 °C (158 °F), cool it before feeding and discard leftovers
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: If you already have safe water, a clean bottle and a kettle, there is no extra equipment to buy ($0 or €0). Allow a few minutes to cool each feed; boiling uses some water and energy.
- In plain language: Powdered infant formula is not sterile and may contain *Cronobacter sakazakii*. Wash your hands, clean and sanitize the feeding equipment, and read the label: some specialty formulas have different temperature limits. For ordinary formula, boil safe drinking water and measure the label's amount into the bottle while the water is still at least 70 °C (158 °F), then add the exact amount of powder and mix. Cool the covered bottle promptly and test a few drops on the inside of your wrist before feeding. Throw away formula left in the bottle after a feed. Infection is rare but can be severe, especially in babies younger than 2 months or born prematurely; babies with low birth weight or weakened immunity need particular care. Ask the baby's clinician whether sterile ready-to-feed liquid formula is a better option.
- Benefit: In a laboratory study, adding powder when the water *inside the bottle* had cooled to about 73.8 °C (165 °F) kept the mixed formula at or above 70 °C in almost all tested conditions. After 30 seconds of passive cooling, *C. sakazakii* counts fell by more than 5 orders of magnitude — over 100,000-fold. This is a reduction in bacteria under test conditions, not a measured reduction in infant deaths. A study of powdered-formula samples repeats a 40–80% fatality estimate for neonatal infection but did not study patient outcomes. In a CDC review of reported invasive infections from 1961–2018, 42 of 112 infants with known outcomes died (38%); this is not the chance that a baby who drinks formula will become infected or die.
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: Beary MA, Daly SE, Baker J, Snyder AB (2025). Assessing Hot Water Reconstitution Instructions and Labeling of Powdered Infant Formula to Ensure Cronobacter spp. Reduction. *Journal of Food Protection*, 88(9), 100571. <https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jfp.2025.100571>; Sima WG, Legesse T, Girma S, et al. (online 2025; volume 2026). Emerging microbial risks: *Cronobacter sakazakii* in powdered infant formula for infants under six months of age in Ethiopia. *BMC Microbiology*, 26, 307. <https://doi.org/10.1186/s12866-025-04380-y>; World Health Organization and FAO (2007). *Safe preparation, storage and handling of powdered infant formula: guidelines*. <https://www.who.int/publications/i/item/9789241595414>. CDC, review of reported invasive infant infections. <https://wwwnc.cdc.gov/eid/article/26/5/19-0858_article>; CDC, prevention and US preparation advice. <https://www.cdc.gov/cronobacter/prevention/>; UK NHS, formula preparation. <https://www.nhs.uk/baby/breastfeeding-and-bottle-feeding/bottle-feeding/making-up-baby-formula/>; US FDA, instructions for specialty formulas. <https://www.fda.gov/media/174643/download?attachment=>.
- Notes: The WHO/FAO guideline recommends water at least 70 °C (158 °F) for powdered formula; the UK NHS also uses this threshold but its kettle-cooling time is specific to its procedure, not a rule for every bottle. In the United States, CDC says following the label is safe in most cases and recommends the hot-water method as an extra precaution for higher-risk babies. Water can cool quickly in a small bottle, so time alone does not guarantee 70 °C at mixing. Some specialty or metabolic formulas must not be prepared this hot; follow their label and the treating clinician's instructions. Do not rely on water that has stood in a flask all day. Use prepared formula within 2 hours, or refrigerate it promptly and use it within 24 hours if it will not be fed right away; once feeding starts, use it within 1 hour and discard the rest. Never leave a prepared bottle at room temperature for the next feed.

### 6. Do not give a baby honey before their first birthday
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: Leaving out honey costs nothing extra: $0 or €0.
- In plain language: Do not give a baby under 1 year old honey on its own, in water, formula or food, in baked goods, or on a pacifier. Honey may contain spores of *Clostridium botulinum*. They can grow in an infant's gut and produce a toxin; adults' natural intestinal defences usually prevent this. Do not offer a baby home-pickled, canned or fermented food if you cannot be sure it was prepared and stored safely.
- Benefit: The US CDC recommends no honey before age 1 because of infant botulism. WHO links spore-contaminated honey to some cases. Avoiding honey removes this preventable source of exposure but cannot prevent every case: spores also occur in the environment. These sources do not quantify a reduction in risk or deaths.
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: US CDC. Preventing Botulism. <https://www.cdc.gov/botulism/prevention/index.html>; current prevention page. <https://www.cdc.gov/botulism/prevention/>; CDC, foods and drinks to avoid or limit for infants. <https://www.cdc.gov/infant-toddler-nutrition/foods-and-drinks/foods-and-drinks-to-avoid-or-limit.html>; World Health Organization. Botulism. <https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/botulism>; UK NHS, foods and drinks to avoid while weaning. <https://www.nhs.uk/best-start-in-life/baby/weaning/safe-weaning/food-and-drinks-to-avoid/>.
- Notes: This includes honey water, honey added to food or formula, honey-containing baked goods and honey pacifiers. Improperly prepared or stored home pickles, preserves and fermented foods can contain toxin already formed in the food. That causes *foodborne* botulism, a different mechanism from infant botulism caused by ingesting spores in honey. Not every fermented food carries the same risk as honey; choose age-appropriate food prepared and stored safely. Salty preserved foods are also unsuitable for babies because of their sodium content.

### 7. Make sure your newborn receives vitamin K at birth
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: If vitamin K is included in covered maternity care or a birth package already paid for, the additional charge is $0 or €0. For separately billed care, ask the hospital for its price; there is no single US or European charge.
- In plain language: Newborn babies have very little vitamin K, which is needed for blood clotting. Without prevention, they can develop serious bleeding, including bleeding in the brain; later cases can cause death or lasting injury. Make sure your baby receives an intramuscular vitamin K shot soon after birth and check that it is recorded before discharge. After a home birth or a birth outside a regular maternity unit, contact a clinician promptly to arrange it. Do not replace the shot with oral drops on your own.
- Benefit: A Belgian consensus recommends a single intramuscular vitamin K dose at birth. It mentions 1 or 2 mg for term infants, but the clinician should follow the local protocol and assess the baby. In Tashkent, Uzbekistan, where routine prevention was not universal, researchers identified 202 cases of vitamin K deficiency bleeding among 42,225 live births in 2014 — 478 per 100,000. A reduction of *more than 78.5%* in that paper is the authors' economic break-even threshold for the prevention program, not an effect measured after the shot.
- Evidence grade: B
- Sources: Fiesack S, Smits A, Rayyan M, et al. (2021). Belgian Consensus Recommendations to Prevent Vitamin K Deficiency Bleeding in the Term and Preterm Infant. *Nutrients*, 13(11), 4109. <https://doi.org/10.3390/nu13114109>; Tursunov D, Yoshida Y, Yrysov K, et al. (2018). Estimated costs for treatment and prophylaxis of newborn vitamin K deficiency bleeding in Tashkent, Uzbekistan. *Nagoya Journal of Medical Science*, 80(1), 11–20. <https://doi.org/10.18999/nagjms.80.1.11>; full study. <https://www.med.nagoya-u.ac.jp/medlib/nagoya_j_med_sci/801/02_Tursuov.pdf>; US CDC, vitamin K and breastfeeding. <https://www.cdc.gov/breastfeeding-special-circumstances/hcp/diet-micronutrients/vitamin-k.html>.
- Notes: An intramuscular shot provides the most reliable protection. A single oral dose is not equivalent. Some countries use clinician-prescribed multi-dose oral regimens if an injection is declined; these depend on completing every dose and on adequate absorption. US CDC recommends one intramuscular dose within 6 hours of birth for both breastfed and formula-fed infants and does not recommend oral vitamin K as a substitute. In Europe, protocols vary by country; the Belgian consensus is not a Europe-wide dosing rule. If you cannot confirm that your newborn received vitamin K, ask a clinician promptly.

### 8. Get urgent medical help if a baby under 3 months has a temperature of 38 °C or higher
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: Seek care now rather than waiting for a cheaper appointment. The extra charge may be $0 or €0 when the visit and tests are covered; US insurance, private care and European copayments vary, so no single out-of-pocket figure is reliable.
- In plain language: If your baby is under 3 months old and has a temperature of 38 °C (100.4 °F) or higher, contact a clinician or urgent service immediately for prompt assessment. In the United States, the American Academy of Pediatrics advises going to an emergency department; in the United Kingdom, NHS advice includes urgent GP care or NHS 111, with emergency care for danger signs. If timely assessment is unavailable, go to an emergency department. Do not wait at home because the baby seems alert, or give fever medicine and wait to see whether the temperature falls. If the baby is hard to wake, has trouble breathing, has a non-fading rash or seems seriously unwell, call emergency services.
- Benefit: The American Academy of Pediatrics' clinical guideline covers **well-appearing, term infants aged 8–60 days** with a documented rectal temperature of at least 38.0 °C. It sets different assessment paths for ages 8–21, 22–28 and 29–60 days because an infant can have a serious bacterial infection without looking obviously ill. Infants younger than 8 days and those aged 61 days to under 3 months fall outside that particular algorithm; NICE separately places **all children under 3 months** with a temperature of at least 38 °C in a high-risk group. These guidelines do not quantify a mortality reduction from seeking care.
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: Pantell RH, Roberts KB, Adams WG, et al. (2021). Evaluation and Management of Well-Appearing Febrile Infants 8 to 60 Days Old. *Pediatrics*, 148(2), e2021052228. <https://doi.org/10.1542/peds.2021-052228>; American Academy of Pediatrics, when to call a pediatrician. <https://www.healthychildren.org/English/health-issues/conditions/fever/Pages/When-to-Call-the-Pediatrician.aspx>; American Academy of Pediatrics, when to go to the emergency department. <https://www.healthychildren.org/English/family-life/health-management/Pages/urgent-care-ER-or-pediatrician-a-parent-guide.aspx>; NICE, *Fever in under 5s*, recommendation 1.2.12. <https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/NG143/chapter/recommendations>; NHS, fever in children. <https://www.nhs.uk/symptoms/fever-in-children/>.
- Notes: The AAP algorithm requires a documented **rectal** temperature; it is a clinician's pathway, not a reason to leave younger, older, premature or ill-looking babies at home. UK NICE recommends a digital thermometer in the armpit for infants under 4 weeks, and the NHS advises this method for young babies. Do not postpone a call while repeatedly measuring. Fever following vaccination still needs age-appropriate clinical judgment; if unsure, ask promptly. A fall in temperature after fever medicine does not rule out a serious infection. Ask a clinician before giving fever medicine to a baby this young; the need for assessment comes first.

### 9. Never shake a baby, however tired or angry you feel
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=是 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: $0 or €0; planning how to take a short break and asking someone to help takes a little time.
- In plain language: Violent shaking can injure a baby's brain and eyes and can cause death or lifelong disability. Gentle rocking to soothe a baby is different. If the crying is pushing you toward losing control, place the baby **on their back in an empty, safe crib**, step into another room for a few minutes, and ask a trusted person to help. A few minutes of crying in a safe crib is safer than shaking. Return and check the baby once you are calm.
- Benefit: The US CDC identifies abusive head trauma from violent shaking or impact as a preventable cause of brain injury and child-abuse deaths. Bleeding around the brain or behind the eyes, lasting disability and death can result. It advises putting the baby somewhere safe, walking away to calm down and checking every 5–10 minutes. This source does not measure the mortality reduction from this specific break-taking step.
- Evidence grade: C
- Sources: US CDC, abusive head trauma and prevention. <https://www.cdc.gov/child-abuse-neglect/about/about-abusive-head-trauma.html>; NHS, soothing a crying baby and avoiding shaking. <https://www.nhs.uk/baby/caring-for-a-newborn/soothing-a-crying-baby/>; American Academy of Pediatrics, responding to a baby's cries. <https://www.healthychildren.org/English/ages-stages/baby/crying-colic/Pages/Responding-to-Your-Babys-Cries.aspx>.
- Notes: Babies have relatively large heads and weak neck muscles; violent shaking moves the head abruptly. If another caregiver takes over, tell them the same rule. If you think a baby has been shaken, seek emergency medical help even if there are no obvious marks. Persistent crying may also signal illness; see entry 8 above. For dealing with intense emotions, see [chapter 3](03_use_your_energy_well.md).

### 10. Choose diapers by fit, timely changes and product-safety notices, not brand prestige
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: At the prices checked on 25 September 2026, a US pack of 120 size-1 diapers was $27.97 and a German pack of 42 size-2 diapers was €4.15. For an illustrative 180–360 changes a month, buying whole packs would cost about **$56–$84** or **€21–€37** at those unit prices, before shipping or promotions. Actual use and prices vary by age, fit and shop; these are examples, not brand recommendations.
- In plain language: Start with a small pack in the right size. See whether it leaves deep marks, leaks or irritates the skin, and whether the fit still works after about a week; then decide whether to buy more. Change a wet or soiled diaper promptly, clean and dry the skin gently, and check the exact product and batch against safety notices. A higher price or an imported label is not proof that a diaper suits your baby or is free of defects.
- Benefit: Frequent changes and a proper fit can prevent some diaper rash. The NHS lists prolonged contact with urine or stool, friction, infrequent changes, irritants and some diaper materials among its possible causes; an allergy or infection may also matter. No regulator cited here ranks all brands from “best” to “worst.” US CPSC recalls and the EU Safety Gate report specific unsafe products, not a general quality score. A June 2026 Chinese government page announced an investigation into a formamide concern involving infant diapers; the cited page does not establish a conclusion about any brand.
- Evidence grade: C

### 11. Borrow, buy used, then buy new for suitable baby gear; check safety before saving
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: Borrowing a safe crib frame may avoid buying one: a new example listed on 25 September 2026 was **$169.99** in the United States or **€59** in Germany, with the mattress sold separately. The saving is the purchase you actually avoid, minus any cleaning, repair, delivery or replacement costs; used-market prices vary.
- In plain language: Do not buy every stroller, crib, wrap and toy in advance. Many baby items are used for only a short period, and used ones can be readily available, though supply depends on where you live. For items your family needs, ask whether you can borrow a sound, current model, then compare a used one with a new one. Check that a used crib or stroller has all its parts and instructions and has not been recalled. Have a safe sleep space ready before the baby arrives; the order of shopping must not delay safety essentials.
- Benefit: Borrowing or buying suitable gear used can reduce spending, but there is no reliable universal saving of “thousands” across US and European markets. For the listed crib example, borrowing its safe frame avoids a new-frame purchase of $169.99 or €59; the baby still needs a suitable mattress. A child car seat protects against fatal injury in a crash, as described in [chapter 1, entry 10](01_avoid_dying_early.md#10-use-a-child-car-seat-for-children-under-four-do-not-hold-them-in-your-arms). Spending less on equipment is worthwhile only when the equipment remains safe.
- Evidence grade: C
