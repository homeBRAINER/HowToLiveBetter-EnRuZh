# 20. Caring for a newborn

This chapter tracks infant mortality and money. It covers only a few measures with substantial effects and strong evidence; day-to-day childcare details are beyond this book's scope. For things to arrange before birth and on the day you leave the hospital, see [chapter 27](../../book/27-怀孕和生产.md), currently available in Chinese.

Translation in progress: the introduction and entries 1–3 of 11 are below. The remaining entries are available in the [Chinese chapter](../../book/20-刚出生的孩子怎么带.md).

### 1. Put your baby to sleep on their back, on a firm, flat surface, in a separate crib or bassinet in your room
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=是 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: No extra cost if you already have a suitable sleep space. If you need a mattress, examples observed on 25 September 2026 were about $100 for a US full-size crib mattress or €50 for a 60 × 120 cm mattress in Germany; neither price includes the crib.
- In plain language: Put your baby on their back for every sleep. Use a firm, flat, non-inclined surface in your room, but give the baby their own sleep space. Keep pillows, blankets, bumpers, stuffed toys and head-shaping pillows out of it. An inclined bassinet or infant seat is not a regular place to sleep. About 3,500 infants a year have died from sleep-related causes in the United States; safe-sleep practices help reduce the risk.
- Benefit: About 3,500 US infants a year have died from sleep-related causes: sudden infant death syndrome, deaths with an unknown cause and accidental suffocation in bed. The American Academy of Pediatrics recommends placing babies on their backs on a firm, non-inclined sleep surface, sharing a room but not a bed, and avoiding soft bedding and overheating. Other risk-reduction measures include breastfeeding, avoiding nicotine and alcohol exposure, keeping up with routine vaccinations and offering a pacifier.
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: Moon RY, Carlin RF, Hand I, et al. (2022). Sleep-Related Infant Deaths: Updated 2022 Recommendations for Reducing Infant Deaths in the Sleep Environment. Pediatrics, 150(1), e2022057990. <https://doi.org/10.1542/peds.2022-057990>; American Academy of Pediatrics, safe-sleep guidance. <https://www.healthychildren.org/English/ages-stages/baby/sleep/Pages/a-parents-guide-to-safe-sleep.aspx>; CDC, US sleep-related infant death figures. <https://www.cdc.gov/mmwr/volumes/67/wr/mm6701e1.htm>. Mattress price examples, 25 September 2026: United States — <https://www.target.com/p/-/A-1013971755>; Germany — <https://www.ikea.com/de/de/p/krummelur-schaummatratze-babybett-20593406/>.
- Notes: Keeping soft objects out includes nursing pillows, even if they look safe. Do not use an inclined bassinet or infant seat as a routine sleep space. If the baby falls asleep in a seat, move them to an appropriate sleep surface as soon as practical. This rule may be hard to maintain when older relatives advise otherwise. The 3,500 figure describes the US burden of sleep-related deaths, not deaths prevented by this single measure. A listed mattress price is not proof that a product is safe: check firmness, a flat surface and a snug fit in the crib.

### 2. Check the hepatitis B birth dose before discharge; WHO recommends it within 24 hours
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: The vaccine in China's national immunization program is free. No equipment purchase ($0/€0); any charge for clinical care in the United States or Europe depends on the country and coverage. Ask the maternity team about your baby's entitlement and schedule.
- In plain language: Check the hepatitis B vaccination plan before your baby leaves the hospital. WHO recommends the first dose as soon as possible after birth, within 24 hours. Infection acquired in infancy or early childhood becomes chronic in about 95% of cases, compared with less than 5% for infections acquired in adulthood. If the mother tests positive for hepatitis B surface antigen (HBsAg), tell the birth team: the baby also needs hepatitis B immune globulin (HBIG) promptly after birth.
- Benefit: WHO recommends that all infants receive the hepatitis B vaccine as soon as possible after birth, within 24 hours. Infection acquired in infancy or early childhood becomes chronic in about 95% of cases; infection acquired in adulthood does so in less than 5%. That difference makes early protection particularly valuable.
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: World Health Organization. Hepatitis B fact sheet, 28 July 2026. <https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/hepatitis-b>; US CDC. Hepatitis B birth-dose policy, 16 December 2025. <https://cdc.gov/media/releases/2025/fact-sheet-hepatitis-b-immunization.html>; US CDC. Clinical Overview of Perinatal Hepatitis B. <https://www.cdc.gov/hepatitis-b/hcp/perinatal-provider-overview/>; European Centre for Disease Prevention and Control. Hepatitis B vaccination policies in EU/EEA countries. <https://www.ecdc.europa.eu/en/publications-data/hepatitis-b-vaccination-policies-practices-and-challenges-achieving-hepatitis>.
- Notes: Have the mother's HBsAg result available before delivery. In US guidance, a baby born to an HBsAg-positive mother receives both the vaccine and HBIG within 12 hours, at separate injection sites. **United States:** since December 2025, when the mother tests negative, the CDC recommends an individual decision with a clinician about giving the birth dose or starting the series later; a positive or unknown maternal status calls for prompt protection under the CDC pathway. **Europe:** national schedules differ; follow the maternity team's schedule, especially if the mother has hepatitis B. Maternal testing is also discussed in [chapter 1](01_avoid_dying_early.md).

### 3. Follow your child's vaccination schedule and catch up on missed doses
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=中 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: Vaccines in China's national immunization program cost the family nothing: the law bars vaccination clinics from charging for them. In the United States, an eligible child can receive a Vaccines for Children (VFC) vaccine for $0, though a provider may charge an administration or other visit fee; the child cannot be refused a VFC vaccine because the family cannot pay the administration fee. In Germany, statutory insurance covers recommended childhood vaccinations, so the covered vaccine itself costs the insured family €0. Entitlement and charges elsewhere in Europe vary. Expect several visits as your child grows, and keep their vaccination record.
- In plain language: Check your country's current schedule and track each dose your child receives. If an appointment is missed, ask the vaccination clinic for a catch-up plan. China's program for children under 18 and the US CDC's catch-up guidance say a delayed multidose series does not need to start over: give the missing doses. China permits the same type of vaccine from another manufacturer when the original product is unavailable; elsewhere, check the interchangeability rules for that specific vaccine. Stable eczema, hives, allergic rhinitis or asthma, and ordinary newborn jaundice are not by themselves reasons to put off every vaccination.
- Benefit: In China, nurseries and schools must check children's vaccination records on enrollment, report missing national-program doses to a vaccination clinic, and help prompt parents to catch up. The 2026 Chinese schedule includes human papillomavirus (HPV) vaccination in the national program. It moved the first diphtheria, tetanus and pertussis (DTaP) dose from 3 months to 2 months, with 5 doses: one each at 2, 4, 6 and 18 months and at 6 years. Since March 2026, eligible children in Tibet, Qinghai, Xinjiang and the Xinjiang Production and Construction Corps have also received routine Japanese encephalitis vaccination. National-program vaccines may be given at the same visit under the routine or catch-up schedule: injections go into different sites and are never mixed in one syringe. If two injectable live attenuated vaccines are not given on the same day, separate them by at least 28 days. China's program does not require an interval between its inactivated or oral live attenuated vaccines and other vaccines when they are given separately. For children under 18, catch up on missing doses as soon as possible without restarting the series; if necessary, continue with the same vaccine type from a different manufacturer. These dates and enrollment rules describe China, not a US or Europe-wide schedule.
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: Standing Committee of the National People's Congress (2019). Vaccine Administration Law of China, articles 48–49. <http://ynswsjkw.yn.gov.cn/html/2019/faguiguizhangxin_0731/6509.html>; government publication of the law. <https://www.samr.gov.cn/zw/zfxxgk/fdzdgknr/fgs/art/2023/art_dde03480953841a5912c864dc29003d0.html>. China's National Disease Control and Prevention Administration and National Health Commission (2026). Q&A on the 2026 national childhood immunization schedule. <https://www.ndcpa.gov.cn/jbkzzx/c100013/common/content/content_2073002930292035584.html>; reprint by the Liaoning Provincial Health Commission. <https://wsjk.ln.gov.cn/wsjk/index/syxwxx/zcjd/2026080900053928035/index.shtml>. US CDC: child and adolescent catch-up schedule, vaccination contraindications, and VFC costs. <https://www.cdc.gov/vaccines/hcp/imz-schedules/child-adolescent-catch-up.html>; <https://www.cdc.gov/vaccines/hcp/imz-best-practices/contraindications-precautions.html>; <https://www.cdc.gov/vaccines-for-children/vfc-information-for-parents/index.html>. European Centre for Disease Prevention and Control: national vaccine scheduler. <https://vaccine-schedule.ecdc.europa.eu/Scheduler/ByCountry>. German Federal Ministry of Health: coverage of recommended vaccinations. <https://www.bundesgesundheitsministerium.de/themen/praevention/impfungen/schutzimpfungen/>.
- Notes: China's 2026 guidance says a family history of allergies, food or medication allergies, or an unspecific label of “allergic” is not itself a contraindication. A child whose eczema, hives, allergic rhinitis or asthma is stable may be vaccinated even while taking oral allergy medicine or inhaled corticosteroids; physiological or breast-milk jaundice in a newborn is not itself a reason to defer. A previous acute severe allergic reaction to the same vaccine, or a severe allergy to one of its components, is a contraindication to the relevant vaccine. Immunodeficiency, chemotherapy, radiotherapy and immunosuppressive treatment require an individual assessment of the child's condition and vaccine type; some doses must wait during intensive treatment. If jaundice accompanies a serious illness requiring hospital care, vaccination may also wait until the child is stable. See [entry 2](#2-check-the-hepatitis-b-birth-dose-before-discharge-who-recommends-it-within-24-hours) for the hepatitis B birth dose. In China, vaccines outside the national program may carry both a vaccine charge and an administration fee. In the US and Europe, use the local schedule and payment rules; Chinese school checks and vaccine dates do not transfer across borders.
