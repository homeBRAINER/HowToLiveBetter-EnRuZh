# 17. Caring for older family members

Money and personal freedom are at stake here. Many problems begin because a family did not discuss decisions or complete the necessary documents while there was still time.

### 1. While an older person can still decide, agree who may help later and put the arrangement in a valid form
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: The conversation and a list of wishes are free: $0 or €0. Creating a legally effective document may cost money; the form and any fee depend on the country and the service used. Chinese law requires a written advance guardian designation but does not make notarization compulsory.
- In plain language: Ask whom the person trusts to help with medical choices, everyday affairs and money if they later cannot decide for themselves. Discuss the responsibilities with that person and record the choice in a form valid where you live. A family conversation, an informal note or access to a bank card does not by itself confer authority to sign on someone else's behalf. Plan particularly early when the person lives alone, relatives live far away or family relationships are complicated.
- Benefit: Under Article 33 of China's Civil Code, an adult with full civil capacity may agree in advance with a close relative or another willing person or organization and **name a future guardian in writing**. That guardian acts if the adult loses civil capacity fully or partly. Without this arrangement, the code provides an order of eligible guardians, permits agreement among them and sets out a dispute process; **a court appointment is not automatic in every case**. Recording the person's choice can prevent a family dispute over authority, though institutions must still check the resulting powers.
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: [China's Civil Code (2020), Articles 28, 30, 31 and 33](https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/fl/202006/t20200602_463888.shtml); [full Civil Code on the Supreme People's Court website](https://www.court.gov.cn/zixun/xiangqing/233181.html); [US Consumer Financial Protection Bureau: financial powers of attorney](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/ask-cfpb/what-is-a-power-of-attorney-poa-en-1149/); [US National Library of Medicine: health-care agents do not control finances](https://medlineplus.gov/ency/patientinstructions/000469.htm); [England and Wales: lasting powers of attorney](https://www.gov.uk/power-of-attorney); [German Federal Ministry of Justice: Vorsorgevollmacht](https://www.bmj.de/DE/themen/vorsorge_betreuungsrecht/vorsorgevollmacht/vorsorgevollmacht_node.html).
- Notes: The Chinese advance guardian designation is a Chinese legal instrument, not a universal form. In the US, a financial power of attorney and a health-care directive can cover different decisions, with state-specific requirements. England and Wales use separate lasting powers of attorney for health and welfare and for property and finances; Scotland and Northern Ireland have different procedures. In Germany, a valid Vorsorgevollmacht for the relevant tasks can avoid the need for court-appointed Betreuung; certain transactions require certification or notarization. Choose the right document and a trustworthy person before paying for any service. Notarization may help prove a Chinese arrangement, but Article 33 itself specifies writing rather than a mandatory notarial act.

### 2. Make a legally valid will, and update it when your wishes change
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: Listing assets and wishes yourself costs $0 or €0. The cost of a legally valid will or professional help depends on local requirements and your circumstances. A free handwritten Chinese will is not automatically valid in the US or across Europe.
- In plain language: Make a will while you can still express your wishes clearly. Check the signing rules where the will will take effect: an error in form can defeat it. In China, a handwritten will must be written throughout, signed and dated by the will-maker; a printed will needs at least two witnesses, and the will-maker and witnesses must sign **every page** and date it. Heirs, recipients of legacies and people with an interest in their inheritance cannot act as witnesses. If several valid wills conflict, the latest governs the conflicting provisions.
- Benefit: China's Civil Code recognizes **6 forms** of will: handwritten, written by another person, printed, audio/video, oral in an emergency, and notarized. Each has its own formalities. An oral emergency will becomes invalid once the emergency ends and the person can make a written or audio/video will. Under Article 1142, the last of several conflicting **valid** wills prevails; since the code took effect in **2021**, an earlier notarized will no longer automatically overrides a later valid will in another form.
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: [China's Civil Code (2020), Articles 1134–1142](https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/fl/202006/t20200602_463888.shtml); [full Civil Code on the Supreme People's Court website](https://www.court.gov.cn/zixun/xiangqing/233181.html); [England and Wales: legal requirements for making a will](https://www.gov.uk/make-will/make-sure-your-will-is-legal); [German Federal Ministry of Justice: making a will](https://www.bmj.de/DE/themen/gesellschaft_familie/erbrecht/erbrecht_node.html).
- Notes: In China, the handwritten text needs the will-maker's signature and the year, month and day. For a printed will, the will-maker and at least **2 witnesses sign each page** and add the date. Article 1140 also excludes people without capacity to witness, heirs, recipients of legacies and others with an interest in those recipients. A later **invalid** document does not simply displace an earlier valid will. Rules elsewhere differ: in England and Wales the will-maker signs a written will in the presence of **2 adult witnesses**, who then sign in the will-maker's presence; a witness or their spouse cannot inherit under that will. German law allows a will entirely handwritten and signed by the testator or a notarial will. US requirements vary by state. Check compulsory inheritance rights and cross-border property with a qualified local adviser.

### 3. Keep an older person's money separate and agree on a pause before large purchases
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: $0 or €0 for a conversation and a simple spending record, without a paid service.
- In plain language: Sellers of dubious health products, collectibles and investments may build trust for months before asking for a large payment. Suggest a voluntary rule: “Before I spend more than an amount I choose, I'll discuss it with someone I trust.” That creates time to check the offer without pressure. Offering to keep a shared record of family expenses is often easier than telling someone they are likely to be deceived.
- Benefit: A separate account and an agreed second check can make an unusual payment visible and delay a decision made under pressure. This is a practical safeguard; no study here measures how much this particular family rule reduces fraud.
- Evidence grade: C
- Sources: Author's experience; no direct study of this rule. See [Chapter 8](08_protect_your_rights_and_property.md) for protecting money and responding to scams and [Chapter 13](13_emergencies.md) for emergencies; [US Securities and Exchange Commission: warning signs of investment scams targeting older people](https://www.investor.gov/introduction-investing/general-resources/news-alerts/alerts-bulletins/investor-alerts/spotting-and-reporting-investment-scams-targeting-older-investors).
- Notes: Agree on the rule with the older person and preserve their control over their own money. Handing all their funds to children “for safekeeping” can create abuse and family disputes. Set up any account access or authorization through the lawful local process.

### 4. Prepare a sentence that lets an older person leave a high-pressure sales pitch
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: $0 or €0; one short conversation and a little practice.
- In plain language: It is hard to argue and calculate under pressure. Agree on a ready answer: “I need to talk this over at home first.” Then leave, end the call and check the offer in peace. The sentence gives the person a way out without arguing on the spot.
- Benefit: A pause interrupts an attempt to secure an immediate commitment. It is a sensible practical tactic, but no direct study here quantifies its protective effect.
- Evidence grade: C
- Sources: Author's experience; no direct study of this exact phrase. [US Securities and Exchange Commission: high-pressure sales tactics are an investment-fraud warning sign](https://www.investor.gov/introduction-investing/general-resources/news-alerts/alerts-bulletins/investor-alerts/spotting-and-reporting-investment-scams-targeting-older-investors).
- Notes: Pair the phrase with the spending agreement in item 3. Also make the home safer: [item 13 of Chapter 1](01_avoid_dying_early.md) covers fall prevention for older people.

### 5. Avoid “retirement investments” that promise returns for a care-home deposit or membership
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: Checking an offer and declining a suspicious deal costs $0 or €0. Tell the older person the common labels: “VIP membership”, “reserved bed”, “care-home share”, “retirement apartment”, “wellness trip” and “health product with cashback”. Do not pay during a free talk, tour or check-up, and do not leave contact details with a seller you have not checked.
- In plain language: If someone wants a large payment now in exchange for investment income or a “guaranteed” place in a future care facility, stop and find out what is actually being sold, who holds the money and what backs the promise. Fraudsters present investment fundraising as care fees, a bed reservation or a product purchase. In two Chinese prosecutions, a supposed apartment-bed reservation collected **CNY 136 million** from more than **2,100 people**, while an “elder-care industry” investment collected **CNY 13.207 billion** from over **110,000 people**. An ordinary advance payment for a real service is not automatically illegal: the warning combination is broad fundraising, a promised return and unclear security for the money.
- Benefit: China's Ministry of Civil Affairs identified **5 recurring forms** of illegal fundraising in elder care: a fake provider or one borrowing another facility's name; “care services” sold through VIP, membership or prepaid cards far beyond available beds; “projects” involving a wellness base, fictitious retirement apartment, long-term bed lease or “time bank”, often with repurchase, sale-and-leaseback or principal-return promises; “senior products” marketed through buybacks, consignment, rebates, free medical checks, gifts, meetings, wellness lectures or supposed expert consultations; and cheap or free “retirement travel” sold with stored-value credits, rewards or investment returns. Under Article **25** of China's regulation against illegal fundraising, organizers must return collected funds, but **participants bear losses that cannot be recovered**; full reimbursement is not guaranteed. In the Dai Mou-ping (戴某平) case, more than **2,100 victims** put in **CNY 136 million** for purported retirement-apartment beds; Dai received **15 years** in prison and a **CNY 400,000** fine. Cao Mou-ming (曹某铭) gathered **CNY 13.207 billion** from over **110,000 people** for purported elder-care investments and received a life sentence, lifelong deprivation of political rights and confiscation of all personal property. In a separate “health product” fraud, Ni Mou-rong (倪某荣) used free meals, gifts and rides to bring older people to sales talks; speakers invented identities and cases, used inflated “discounts” and falsely claimed that dihydroquercetin could prevent or fight cancer. Sales exceeded **CNY 620,000**, at many times the product's cost; the sentence was **10 years 3 months** and a **CNY 120,000** fine. The Chinese regulation took effect on **1 May 2021**.
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: [China's Ministry of Civil Affairs (2023): five illegal-fundraising patterns in elder care](https://www.gov.cn/lianbo/bumen/202306/content_6886556.htm); [Ministry of Civil Affairs and banking regulator (2021): elder-care fundraising warning](https://www.mca.gov.cn/n152/n164/c36171/content.html); [China's State Council (2021): Regulation No. 737 on illegal fundraising, Articles 2, 25 and 30](https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/2021-02/10/content_5586632.htm); [Supreme People's Procuratorate (2022): elder-fraud cases](https://www.spp.gov.cn/xwfbh/wsfbt/202206/t20220617_560010.shtml); [Supreme People's Court (2025): Ni Mou-rong case](https://www.court.gov.cn/zixun/xiangqing/482861.html); [US Securities and Exchange Commission: investment-scam warning signs for older investors](https://www.investor.gov/introduction-investing/general-resources/news-alerts/alerts-bulletins/investor-alerts/spotting-and-reporting-investment-scams-targeting-older-investors).
- Notes: Check the promised service and return rather than the provider's respectable appearance. Lawful prepayment for actual care is different from collecting public investments with a return promise. A free lecture, trip or consultation is not a crime by itself; the critical moment is paying. Combine this check with the voluntary rule in item **3**. If money has gone, contact the bank and report the suspected fraud promptly; [item 2 of Chapter 8](08_protect_your_rights_and_property.md) gives the first steps. China's **110** and **96110** emergency and anti-fraud numbers apply in China, not in the US or Europe. For a scam using a home as collateral, see the next item.

### 6. Do not borrow against your home to buy an “income for retirement” investment
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: Declining a suspicious offer costs $0 or €0. Keep property documents away from an unsolicited seller, and give yourself time for independent advice before signing any mortgage, guarantee or loan. A discussion with trusted relatives can help, but the homeowner makes the decision.
- In plain language: In this scam, an older owner is pushed into mortgaging a home and investing the borrowed money in a supposedly safe high-return product. Early “returns” may come from later investors. The owner can end up with a secured debt, lost savings and even a lost home. This is **different from** a properly disclosed reverse mortgage or other lawful equity-release product. Never mortgage a home just to buy a seller's investment, and never sign a blank contract.
- Benefit: In **June 2021**, China's banking and insurance regulator described housing-reverse-mortgage pension insurance as the one official Chinese product of this kind and distinguished it from scams marketed as “housing for retirement”. In the Chinese insurance arrangement described then, an older person with lawful title pledged the home to an **insurance company**, retained possession, use and income rights, could receive payments under the contract and needed the mortgagee's consent to dispose of the property. Fraud promoters instead steered owners into mortgages to fund their recommended investments, often a Ponzi scheme paying earlier participants with new money. Some owners reportedly did not know their property had been mortgaged until they had lost the home and still owed the loan. The regulator suggested questioning promised returns above **6%**, treating more than **8%** as very dangerous and preparing to lose the entire principal above **10%**. The **6%** marker was a prompt to investigate, not proof of a crime. These were **2021 Chinese consumer-warning heuristics**, not legal thresholds or universal risk cutoffs for US or European investments.
- Evidence grade: B
- Sources: [China's banking and insurance regulator (2021): investment and housing-for-retirement fraud warning, reproduced by Hunan's financial authority](https://dfjrjgj.hunan.gov.cn/dfjrjgj/yhlj/202106/t20210608_19450856.html); [Ministry of Civil Affairs and banking regulator (2021): fundraising promoted as housing-for-retirement](https://www.mca.gov.cn/n152/n164/c36171/content.html); [China's State Council: formation of the National Administration of Financial Regulation in 2023](https://english.www.gov.cn/news/202305/18/content_WS6465bd1ac6d03ffcca6ed27f.html); [US Consumer Financial Protection Bureau: reverse-mortgage risks](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/ask-cfpb/what-is-a-reverse-mortgage-en-224/); [US Consumer Financial Protection Bureau: age and counseling requirements for a Home Equity Conversion Mortgage](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/ask-cfpb/can-anyone-take-out-a-reverse-mortgage-loan-en-227/); [UK Financial Conduct Authority: checking a firm offering home finance or equity release](https://www.fca.org.uk/consumers/how-check-firm-individual-authorised).
- Notes: Grade B reflects a regulator's risk warning and practical warning signs, not a measured effectiveness study. The **6%**, **8%** and **10%** figures are not a legal test for fraud. The former China Banking and Insurance Regulatory Commission was replaced by the National Administration of Financial Regulation in 2023. Its **2021** notice does not establish whether its product was still a pilot in **2026**. In the US, a Home Equity Conversion Mortgage is a regulated reverse-mortgage product for eligible owners aged **62 or older**, with HUD-approved counseling; it is still a loan that accrues interest and fees. In the UK, lifetime mortgages are an equity-release product subject to separate rules and advice. Check the actual lender, adviser and terms under local law. Do not sign blank documents; see [item 17 of Chapter 8](08_protect_your_rights_and_property.md), including [item 2](08_protect_your_rights_and_property.md) if a payment has already been made.

### 7. If an older person needs sustained care, check the services and coverage where they live
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=中 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: Under China's scheme, a person applies and undergoes an assessment of loss of independence. When the first assessment confirms eligibility, the fund generally pays that assessment fee; other assessments may involve a personal charge. In the US and Europe, eligibility checks, insurance premiums and care charges depend on the applicable system, so ask the local provider before paying.
- In plain language: If someone cannot manage basic daily activities for a prolonged period, find out what support is available before paying every care bill yourself. In China, apply to the long-term care insurance office where the person is insured. At the start of the scheme, it covers people with severe loss of independence that has usually lasted **at least 6 months**, subject to contributions and an approved assessment. In the US, ask about Medicaid eligibility and care services; ordinary Medicare does **not** pay for long-term custodial care. In Germany, ask the Pflegekasse for a care-needs assessment and the benefits linked to the assigned Pflegegrad. These are distinct programmes, not applications of China's rules.
- Benefit: China's **2026** long-term care scheme has no initial deductible for **covered care services**. The fund pays roughly **70%** for people insured under employee rules and roughly **50%** for non-employed urban and rural residents. The annual fund-payment ceiling is at most **50%** of the previous year's per-capita disposable income in the relevant region. These are baseline parameters of the Chinese scheme, not promised US or European reimbursement rates.
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: [China's National Healthcare Security Administration and seven other agencies (2026): implementation plan for long-term care insurance](https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202603/content_7063915.htm); [Communist Party Central Committee and State Council (2026): framework for long-term care insurance](https://www.gov.cn/gongbao/2026/issue_12666/202604/content_7065119.html); [US Medicare: long-term custodial care is not covered, while Medicaid may help eligible people](https://www.medicare.gov/coverage/long-term-care); [German Federal Health Ministry: long-term care insurance benefits in 2026](https://www.bundesgesundheitsministerium.de/themen/pflege/online-ratgeber-pflege/leistungen-der-pflegeversicherung/leistungen-im-ueberblick/).
- Notes: In China, the loss of independence generally needs to persist for **at least 6 months**; a few weeks in hospital is not enough by itself. The scheme initially focuses on severe need and requires an application and assessment. Someone who joined late or resumed contributions may face a fixed waiting period, generally **6 months**, subject to local rules. The care fund excludes accommodation and meals, as well as medical costs payable by ordinary health insurance; someone receiving a work-injury living-care payment cannot receive overlapping benefits. Home and community care may qualify. Implementation is phased by region: check with the person's local Chinese insurance office before applying. Do not apply China's **70%/50%** rates to Medicare, Medicaid or German Pflegeversicherung.
