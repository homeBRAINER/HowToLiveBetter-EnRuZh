# 22. Relaxation, entertainment and stress relief

The first half concerns costs and safety at entertainment venues, measured in money and personal freedom. The second gives more specific ways to manage stress, with energy and all-cause mortality as its outcomes. These measures cannot be converted into one another. [Chapter 3](03_use_your_energy_well.md) covers the first things to do when you feel low; this chapter gives more specific steps.

The introduction and the first 4 of 11 entries are translated here. The [complete Chinese text](../../book/22-怎么放松.md) remains available.

### 1. Find the exit when you enter a karaoke venue, bar or escape room; leave if it is locked or obstructed
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: $0 or €0; take about 10 seconds on arrival to locate exit signs and a route out.
- In plain language: Find the exits before your evening begins. If a door on the escape route will not open, objects block the passage, or the exit sign is hidden, choose another venue and tell the staff. In a basement or crowded room, make especially sure you know how to leave: smoke can rapidly impair visibility in a fire, and a narrow passage can become dangerous.
- Benefit: In China, the manager of a licensed entertainment venue is responsible for fire safety, equipment checks and an evacuation plan under Article 20 of the entertainment-venue regulation. While it is open, escape routes and exits must remain clear and unlocked, without barriers or covered signs, under Article 21. A separate rule for Chinese internet cafés, Article 24 of their regulation, forbids blocking or locking doors, windows and escape routes during business hours, as well as using open flames for lighting or allowing smoking. People may report violations at entertainment venues to the responsible authorities; under Article 30, signs must display police and cultural-authority reporting numbers. Russian emergency officials likewise warn against doors that need a key to open from inside or obstructed routes. London Fire Brigade gives the same practical advice for bars and clubs. These rules and recommendations do not quantify how much a ten-second check reduces mortality.
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: State Council of China (2006), Regulations on the Administration of Entertainment Venues, Decree 458, Articles 20–21. <http://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/2008-03/28/content_6297.htm>; current 2020 text, Articles 20, 21, 30 and 37. <https://xzfg.moj.gov.cn/front/law/detail?LawID=1536>. State Council of China (2002), Regulations on the Administration of Commercial Internet Access Service Venues, Decree 363. <http://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2002/content_61788.htm>; current text, Article 24. <https://xzfg.moj.gov.cn/front/law/detail?LawID=944>. Russian Emergency Ministry, guidance on keeping emergency exits clear. <https://43.mchs.gov.ru/deyatelnost/press-centr/garnizonnye-novosti/5647515>. London Fire Brigade, fire safety for nightclubs, bars and pubs. <https://www.london-fire.gov.uk/safety/the-workplace/nightclubs-bars-and-pubs-fire-safety/>. US Fire Administration, review of nightclub fires. <https://apps.usfa.fema.gov/downloads/pdf/statistics/v3i7.pdf>.
- Notes: A basement room, one exit, a cluttered corridor and a failed exit sign are different warning signs. A locked exit or blocked route calls for immediate action; a basement location or a single exit **does not by itself prove** a legal violation. Capacity, design and local rules matter. If a venue feels cramped or overcrowded, or you can find only one unclear way out, choose another. Do not judge an escape room by whether you can solve its puzzle: ask how its door opens in an emergency. China's Decree 458 covers certain licensed song, dance and amusement venues, so it is not automatically the law for every bar or escape room; Russia, Belarus, the US and European countries have their own rules. There is no universal number of seconds available to escape a fire; crowds and alcohol can make evacuation harder.

### 2. Ask for a price list before ordering; do not accept “enjoy yourself now, we'll work out the bill later”
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: Asking for the menu and noting prices costs $0 or €0; it takes a short conversation with staff.
- In plain language: Before ordering a drink, a “special” cocktail, a fruit platter or a paid service, ask its price and about any mandatory extras. Keep the menu or photograph the relevant page if permitted. Ask for a confirmed itemised list after ordering. If the bill differs, request a detailed breakdown and an explanation on the spot; keep the receipt and any messages.
- Benefit: Article 29 of China's entertainment-venue regulation requires venues to display the prices of services and goods, show consumers a price list and refrain from forcing or deceiving them into purchases. Price-law breaches are addressed under Article **54** of the current regulation. Russia requires food-service menus or price lists to make product, portion and price information available; Belarus has its own retail and catering rules. In the EU, traders must give clear information about the total price before a purchase. These provisions protect access to prices. There are no data showing that “almost all” expensive-drink disputes start without a price list.
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: State Council of China (2006), Regulations on the Administration of Entertainment Venues, Decree 458. <http://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/2008-03/28/content_6297.htm>; current text, Articles 29 and 54. <https://xzfg.moj.gov.cn/front/law/detail?LawID=1536>. Russian consumer protection authority, menu and drinks-list requirements for food services. <https://zpp.rospotrebnadzor.ru/news/federal/565057>. Belarusian Tax Ministry, official list of current regulations including Council of Ministers Decree 703 on retail and catering, updated in 2026. <https://nalog.gov.by/tax_control/control_of_goods/tobacco/>. European Union, information about the total price before purchase. <https://europa.eu/youreurope/citizens/consumers/shopping/contract-information/index_en.htm>. Beijing government, consumer complaints through the 12315 platform. <https://www.beijing.gov.cn/fuwu/bmfw/bmjy/202504/t20250409_4060597.html>.
- Notes: In China you can raise a billing dispute through 12315; it is a Chinese service, not a hotline for other countries. In Russia, complaints about consumer rights can go to Rospotrebnadzor; in Belarus, use the competent local consumer authority or MART. First keep the bill and ask the venue to resolve the difference. In the US, exact menu rules and complaint routes vary by state and locality; the Chinese regulation does not apply. In the EU, the European Consumer Centre can help with a cross-border dispute. Paying a bill does not automatically waive your right to dispute an incorrect charge, but without the menu and confirmed order it can be harder to establish what was agreed.

### 3. Leave if someone offers an unknown substance; do not let others use your room to take it
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: $0 or €0; you need to end the outing and leave.
- In plain language: If someone produces an unknown powder, tablet or vape cartridge and asks you to “try it”, decline and leave. Do not stay to watch or agree to let the room be used for taking it. If others may be in danger, alert staff or the police from a safe place.
- Benefit: In China, Article 14 of the entertainment-venue regulation prohibits a licensed venue and its staff from selling or supplying drugs, organising, forcing, encouraging or deceiving people into using them, allowing people to use them, or facilitating those acts. Under Article 43, a venue that breaks this rule can have unlawful proceeds and property confiscated and be ordered to close for **3–6 months** to put matters right; serious cases can cost it its licence and lead to fines for responsible staff. Article 30 requires prominent warnings about drugs, gambling and prostitution, with numbers for reporting violations. For an individual in China, taking a drug, supplying it to someone else and knowingly allowing its use in a place under their control have different consequences: Articles 84–85 of the 2025 public-security law, in force since 2026, provide for administrative penalties, while Criminal Code Article 354 and the Supreme People's Court's Article 12 interpretation set the conditions for a crime of providing a place for drug use. **Booking the room in your name does not automatically make every single incident a crime.**
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: State Council of China (2006), Regulations on the Administration of Entertainment Venues, Decree 458. <http://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/2008-03/28/content_6297.htm>; current text, Articles 14, 30 and 43. <https://xzfg.moj.gov.cn/front/law/detail?LawID=1536>. National People's Congress of China (2025), Public Security Administration Punishments Law, Articles 84–85. <https://www.npc.gov.cn/npc/c2/c30834/202506/t20250627_446254.html>. Supreme People's Court of China (2016), interpretation on drug offences, Article 12. <https://gongbao.court.gov.cn/Details/53c344a8e4b67174a8747677d23060.html>. Criminal Code of China, Article 354. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/fl/201802/t20180206_364975.shtml>.
- Notes: The Chinese rules for licensed karaoke and amusement venues are not law in the US or European countries. Apply local law to questions about supply or allowing drug use on premises. Under the court interpretation, the criminal threshold for providing a place depends, for example, on how many people and occasions were involved, whether a minor was involved, profit or serious harm; control of the premises also matters. Leaving is a practical way to avoid danger, but these laws do not measure how much the advice protects personal freedom.

### 4. Decline food, drinks or vapes of uncertain origin; replace a drink left unattended
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: Declining a doubtful offer costs $0 or €0. Replacing a drink left unattended costs whatever the venue charges; that step is not necessarily free.
- In plain language: Do not try a sweet, drink or vape cartridge of unknown contents offered by a stranger. If you left your drink unattended and are unsure about it, do not finish it; get a fresh one. Spiking is the perpetrator's fault, never the victim's. If you suddenly feel confused or sick, lose coordination or cannot remember what happened, get somewhere safe, ask someone you trust or venue staff for help, and seek medical care promptly. Report suspected spiking to the police: some substances become difficult to detect quite quickly.
- Benefit: China brought synthetic cannabinoids under class-wide control on **1 July 2021**. In cases investigated in Wujiang, liquids containing them could be coloured and flavoured, and the devices looked much like ordinary vapes; sellers offered them in bars, karaoke venues and hotels. Minors accounted for nearly **40%** of the local cases involving these vapes that officials handled in **2021**. This is a figure for those local cases, not a national estimate. On **1 October 2023**, China placed etomidate in its category-II psychotropic-substance list, with an exception for approved medicinal products. Under China's 2025 public-security law, effective **1 January 2026**, inducing, deceiving or forcing another person to take drugs can bring **10–15 days** of administrative detention and a fine of **1,000–5,000 yuan**. Where it is a crime, Criminal Code Article 353 provides up to **3 years'** imprisonment or another stated criminal penalty, **3–7 years** in serious cases, and **3–10 years** for forcing someone to take drugs; offences against minors are punished more severely. For *voluntary unlawful use*, Article 84 provides **10–15 days** of detention and a possible fine of up to **3,000 yuan**; less serious cases can bring up to **5 days** or a fine of up to **1,000 yuan**. A ban on entering entertainment venues for **6 months to 1 year** may also be imposed. A person found to have a drug dependence may be ordered to undergo **3 years** of community rehabilitation; under statutory conditions, compulsory isolation normally lasts **2 years** and may be extended by no more than **1 year**. **None of these measures follows automatically when someone is drugged without their knowledge**: the facts and legal grounds must be established.
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: Supreme People's Procuratorate of China (2021), report on Wujiang cases. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/zdgz/202110/t20211011_531831.shtml>. National Medical Products Administration, Ministry of Public Security and National Health Commission of China (2023), 2023 Notice 120, hosted by the Shanghai drug regulator. <https://yjj.sh.gov.cn/qtgzwj/20230911/3b612c94e35b4b63abba314c972a2593.html>. Standing Committee of the National People's Congress (2025), 2025 Public Security Administration Punishments Law, Articles 20, 84–85 and 113. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/fl/202506/t20250627_699863.shtml>. National People's Congress (1997), Criminal Code, Article 353. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/fl/201802/t20180206_364975.shtml>. Standing Committee of the National People's Congress (2007), Anti-Drug Law, Guangdong government copy, Articles 33, 38 and 47. <http://www.gd.gov.cn/zwgk/wjk/zcfgk/content/post_2520706.html>. Supreme People's Court of China, official Anti-Drug Law text. <https://gongbao.court.gov.cn/Details/6dcc499b2e87f0d38f63d1011abdee.html>. UK government, advice and support after spiking. <https://www.gov.uk/guidance/spiking-advice-and-support>. - Notes: The same precaution applies at a friend's gathering, near a school or on a long journey, not only in a club. Ordinary-looking sweets, drinks and vapes do not reveal their contents; appearance alone proves nothing. In the US, call **911** if you are in immediate danger; in the EU, call **112**. China's fines, detention rules and rehabilitation periods are not US or European law. Spiking can be followed by theft, sexual assault or extortion, but their frequency cannot be inferred from the cited reports. Seek help and report what happened even if you cannot recall every detail. See entry 3 above for unknown substances offered at a venue and [chapter 8, entry 27](08_protect_your_rights_and_property.md) for requests to carry someone else's belongings.
