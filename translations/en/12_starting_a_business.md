# 12. Starting a business: don't put your family's finances at risk

> Partial translation: the introduction and entries 1–17 of 19. The legal rules below concern China, not the US or Europe. Regional guidance will be provided separately.

This chapter is about money and legal liability. When you open a shop or start a company, which decisions put your personal assets, family property and your spouse's assets at risk? What does the law say, and can you keep those risks separate in advance? The benefits here are not converted into years of life. Entries start with the risks most likely to cost you your family's savings and the easiest precautions to take. Decisions about what to sign come first; methods that take more work come later. Advice based on experience receives evidence grade C.

> Legal clarifications, 21 September 2026: the notes distinguish an unpaid capital commitment, a personal guarantee and liability for a company's debts. Chinese legal forms are described on their own terms; an individual business here means China's *geti gongshanghu*. The capital example in CNY illustrates a Chinese legal obligation, not a local market price.

### 1. Start with money you can afford to lose; protect household essentials and don't borrow to launch
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->

- Cost: 0. Before starting, set a limit you could lose completely without missing mortgage payments, compromising your children's needs or giving up medical care. Don't invest beyond it. Exclude borrowed money and money belonging to other family members.
- In plain language: An individually operated business answers for its debts with the operator's personal assets. A family business—or one whose individual and family operations cannot be distinguished—answers with family property. A debt jointly accepted by spouses, or borrowing shown to fund their joint business, can put both spouses on the hook. If a company cannot pay debts that are due, you can also be required to make a promised capital contribution ahead of its deadline. Choose an investment amount you could lose while still paying the mortgage and medical bills.
- Benefit: A failed business can cost more than the cash you put in. Under Chinese law, an individual business's debts are covered by personal assets if operated individually, and by family property if family-run or indistinguishable from family operations. Debts jointly signed for or later ratified by both spouses are joint debts. For borrowing by one spouse beyond ordinary household needs, a creditor who proves that the money supported the spouses' joint business can establish a joint debt. If a company cannot pay due debts, a creditor with a due claim can demand early payment of a shareholder's subscribed but not yet due capital contribution. These rules apply nationally in China.
- Evidence grade: B
- Sources: National People's Congress (2020), Civil Code, Articles 56 and 1064. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/fl/202006/t20200602_463888.shtml> NPC Standing Committee (2023), Company Law, 2023 revision, Articles 47 and 54. <https://www.gov.cn/yaowen/liebiao/202312/content_6923395.htm> Official full texts are also available from [MIIT](https://gdca.miit.gov.cn/zwgk/zcwj/flfg/art/2020/art_573d6ef5018b46b6a4e1f31ca085a710.html) and [SAMR](https://www.samr.gov.cn/djzcj/zcfg/fl/art/2026/art_9b4263a169cf43c2aba950cfc9ac1af2.html).
- Notes: No verifiable original statistics on business survival rates or average lifespan were located on the National Bureau of Statistics or SAMR websites for this entry. It therefore gives legal consequences without a failure-rate estimate and retains grade B. “Affordable to lose” means you would not have to sell your home or borrow from relatives to cover the loss. Your personal spending limit does not itself limit your legal liability; the business structure and documents you sign matter too.

### 2. Avoid personally guaranteeing company loans, and don't ask your spouse to sign too
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->

- Cost: 0. If a bank, supplier or landlord asks for a shareholder's joint and several personal guarantee or a spouse's signature, treat it as a decision about exposing personal assets. Avoid signing if possible. If you do agree to a guarantee, seek a general guarantee with an explicit maximum amount.
- In plain language: A joint and several guarantee can let the creditor claim directly from you when the company fails to pay a due debt or an agreed trigger occurs. A general guarantee normally lets you require the creditor to sue or arbitrate and pursue enforcement against the company first. If your spouse also undertakes the obligation, both of you can be exposed. A personal guarantee creates your own obligation alongside the company's debt.
- Benefit: A guarantee commits you to perform or bear liability when the debtor fails to pay a due debt or another agreed condition occurs. With a joint and several guarantee, the creditor can claim directly from the guarantor within the guarantee's scope. With a general guarantee, the creditor normally must first obtain a judgment or arbitral award and pursue enforcement against the debtor's assets without recovering the debt in full. Spouses' joint acceptance or subsequent ratification of a debt makes it joint. These are national Chinese rules in force since 2021.
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: National People's Congress (2020), Civil Code, Articles 681, 687, 688 and 1064. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/fl/202006/t20200602_463888.shtml> The [official full text](https://gdca.miit.gov.cn/zwgk/zcwj/flfg/art/2020/art_573d6ef5018b46b6a4e1f31ca085a710.html) also sets out the exceptions in Article 687.
- Notes: A small-business lender may require a shareholder's personal guarantee; no verified evidence supports a claim that almost all banks do so. This is advice to understand and limit a guarantee, not a blanket rule against business borrowing. Keep the amount within the affordable-loss limit in entry 1. See chapter 8, entry 16 for loan acknowledgments and guarantees. **Legal clarification:** a general guarantor's right to insist on prior enforcement has exceptions, including an accepted bankruptcy case, evidence of insufficient assets or inability to pay, an untraceable debtor with no enforceable assets, and a written waiver. A spouse's signature matters according to the obligation accepted; signing merely as a witness is not automatically a joint debt. A personal guarantee does not abolish the company's separate legal status.

### 3. Choose the legal structure before opening: individual businesses and general partners risk personal assets; a company can limit liability
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=中 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->

- Cost: Allow a few hours to register through the local government services portal in China. For a limited liability company, the main ongoing cost is the time spent on monthly bookkeeping and required tax filings, or the fee charged by a bookkeeping provider; fees and filing schedules vary. Comparable local service prices have not yet been verified.
- In plain language: An individual business or general partnership can expose assets beyond your investment. A shareholder in a Chinese limited liability company normally commits only the amount they subscribed. That commitment is real: for a newly formed company covered by the general rule, it must be contributed within 5 years. If your own subscribed contribution is 1 million CNY, that is a commitment of 1 million CNY. Capital improperly withdrawn must be returned. Mixing company and personal money can also undermine protection; a sole shareholder who cannot prove the assets are separate can be liable for company debts.
- Benefit: An individual business answers with personal or family property. General partners normally have unlimited joint and several liability; in a limited partnership, limited partners generally answer only up to their subscribed contributions. Shareholders in a limited liability company normally have liability to the company limited to their subscribed contributions. Three qualifications matter: contributions must normally be paid within 5 years of incorporation, and a creditor with a due claim may demand early payment if the company cannot pay due debts; improperly withdrawn capital must be returned; and abusing corporate separation to evade debts and seriously harm creditors, or a sole shareholder's failure to prove separate assets, can result in joint and several liability for company debts. The revised Company Law took effect nationally in China on 1 July 2024.
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: National People's Congress (2020), Civil Code, Article 56. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/fl/202006/t20200602_463888.shtml> NPC Standing Committee (2006), Partnership Enterprise Law, 2006 revision, Article 2. <http://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2006/content_413955.htm> NPC Standing Committee (2023), Company Law, 2023 revision, Articles 4, 23, 47, 53 and 54. <https://www.gov.cn/yaowen/liebiao/202312/content_6923395.htm> See also the official [Partnership Enterprise Law](https://www.npc.gov.cn/zgrdw/npc/xinwen/lfgz/zxfl/2006-08/27/content_351857.htm), [Company Law](https://www.samr.gov.cn/djzcj/zcfg/fl/art/2026/art_9b4263a169cf43c2aba950cfc9ac1af2.html) and [company registration rules, Articles 8–9](https://www.samr.gov.cn/zw/zfxxgk/fdzdgknr/fgs/art/2024/art_6580c00811be45bfa304c1273b74e294.html).
- Notes: Keep company and personal accounts separate, don't pay household bills with company funds, and retain complete records. Don't inflate registered capital for appearances: subscribing 1 million CNY means committing that 1 million CNY, normally within 5 years for a newly formed company. **Legal clarification:** paying promised capital or returning improperly withdrawn capital does not, by itself, abolish limited liability. Your contribution is distinct from the company's total registered capital. Eligible property, as well as money, may be contributed. Companies registered before July 2024 have transitional rules: where the remaining contribution period measured from July 2027 exceeds 5 years, it generally must be shortened to no more than 5 years by the end of June 2027. Specified public-interest cases can receive approval to retain an earlier timetable. Special statutory rules also exist for certain partnerships; the general comparison here is not exhaustive.

### 4. Don't become a shareholder in name only or hold someone else's shares as a favor
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->

- Cost: 0. Turn down requests to “borrow your ID to list you as a shareholder” or “put the shares in your name for now.”
- In plain language: Agreeing to be the registered shareholder can leave you responsible for the capital contribution. You must pay it on time and in full; a missed payment can also mean compensating the company for resulting losses. At incorporation, another founder's failure to contribute as the articles require can make you jointly liable for the shortfall. Someone else may run the business while the contribution obligation is yours.
- Benefit: A shareholder must make the subscribed contribution in full by the deadlines in the articles of association and compensate the company for losses caused by late or incomplete payment. Where a founding shareholder fails to make the contribution required at incorporation, the other founding shareholders are jointly and severally liable within the shortfall. If the company cannot pay due debts, a creditor with a due claim can demand early payment of a subscribed contribution whose deadline has not yet arrived. Agreeing to appear in the register can therefore expose you even when someone else operates the business. These Company Law rules took effect nationally in China on 1 July 2024.
- Evidence grade: B
- Sources: NPC Standing Committee (2023), Company Law, 2023 revision, Articles 47, 49, 50 and 54. <https://www.gov.cn/yaowen/liebiao/202312/content_6923395.htm> [Official consolidated text](https://www.samr.gov.cn/djzcj/zcfg/fl/art/2026/art_9b4263a169cf43c2aba950cfc9ac1af2.html). See also the Supreme People's Court's [Company Law Interpretation III, amended in 2020](https://gongbao.court.gov.cn/Details/3da4e92fb4866e5d5e86617a099b15.html) and its [2020 amending decision](https://gongbao.court.gov.cn/Details/7166c5267fc84d995d5e52b8edbc0c.html).
- Notes: Articles 24 and 25 of Interpretation III address the nominee agreement and disposal of the shares; Article 26 addresses a nominee's liability for unpaid contributions and recourse against the actual investor. An agreement may govern the relationship between those people without protecting the nominee from a creditor's claim. **Verification limit:** the official indexed text of these provisions was available, but the full Supreme People's Court page could not be retrieved; verification of the complete text and its application alongside the revised Company Law remains pending. Grade B is retained. Liability here is not automatically liability for every company debt. Article 50 also covers materially overvalued noncash contributions at incorporation; it does not make every contribution with a future payment date an existing shortfall. See chapter 8, entry 26 for the risks of serving as a nominal legal representative.

### 5. Check the franchise filing, get written disclosures and put the cancellation period in the contract
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=中 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->

- Cost: 0. Search China's Ministry of Commerce franchise information system and request written disclosures and the draft contract before signing. Allow a few hours for these checks.
- In plain language: A franchisor generally needs at least 2 directly operated outlets with more than 1 year of operation. It must file with the commerce authority within 15 days of its first franchise contract. Failure to file brings a fine of 10,000–50,000 CNY; failure to comply with the authority's correction deadline raises that to 50,000–100,000 CNY and public disclosure. Before signing, you must receive 12 categories of written information, including franchise fees, deposit refund conditions, existing outlets' performance, audit summaries for the past 2 years and franchise litigation over the past 5 years. If you cannot find the filing, the company refuses disclosure or it demands payment before showing the contract, walk away.
- Benefit: Under the national Chinese rules in force since May 2007, a franchisor generally must have at least 2 directly operated outlets and more than 1 year of operation, and file within 15 days of its first franchise contract. Failure to file can lead to a fine of 10,000–50,000 CNY; missing the correction deadline set by the authority leads to 50,000–100,000 CNY and public disclosure. Before signing, the franchisor must disclose 12 categories of information: these include the types and amounts of franchise fees, deposit refund conditions, the number and performance of existing franchised outlets, audit report summaries for the past 2 years and franchise litigation and arbitration over the past 5 years. The contract must let the franchisee withdraw unilaterally within a specified period after signing. Concealment or false information can give the franchisee grounds to terminate; the disclosure rules specify concealment that affects performance and prevents the contract's purpose from being achieved, or the provision of false information.
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: State Council (2007), Regulations on the Administration of Commercial Franchises, Order 485, Articles 7, 8, 12, 22, 23 and 25. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/2008-03/28/content_4179.htm> Ministry of Commerce (2012), Measures for the Administration of Information Disclosure in Commercial Franchising, Order 2 of 2012, Articles 5 and 9. <http://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2012/content_2177025.htm> Ministry of Commerce, Commercial Franchise Information Management System. <https://txjy.syggs.mofcom.gov.cn/> Accessible official texts: [franchise regulations](https://www.samr.gov.cn/zw/zfxxgk/fdzdgknr/bgt/art/2023/art_50c8e28de6584adeb6dd5e96e1c29359.html) and [disclosure measures](https://www.mofcom.gov.cn/dl/file/20211203231047.pdf).
- Notes: Treat a missing filing, refusal to provide disclosures or a demand for payment before you can read the contract as reasons to walk away. Specify the number of days in the cancellation period: the regulation itself only requires a defined period. Ask where the figures for existing outlets' average sales, gross profit and net profit come from; the disclosure measures require the source of those figures. **Legal clarification:** the draft contract and disclosures are normally due at least 30 days before signing; the disclosure measures exempt renewals on unchanged terms. The higher filing fine follows a missed official correction deadline, not automatically the 16th day after signing. A missing database entry is a warning sign, not proof of a violation: the filing obligation starts with the first franchise contract. Article 33 also contains a transitional exception to the outlet requirement for businesses already franchising before the regulations took effect.

### 6. Decide on the name, premises, business scope and capital before registering; prepare the full application
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=中 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->

- Cost: China's enterprise and individual-business registration fees have been suspended since 1 January 2015. Registration itself is free. Allow a few hours to apply through the local government services portal or in person. Company seals, bookkeeping services and a registered-address service are market expenses, not government registration fees.
- In plain language: Registration fees have been suspended since 2015. The law sets out 5 categories of documents, with additional requirements for particular business structures. A complete application in the legally required form can be registered on the spot; otherwise the normal deadline is 3 working days. You declare the name yourself and generally subscribe the capital yourself, subject to the applicable rules. A fee quoted by an intermediary may be for handling the application. You do not have to accept a claim that you must use a designated seal maker.
- Benefit: The registration rules list 5 document categories: (1) the application; (2) documents establishing the applicant's eligibility and a natural person's identification; (3) documents for the domicile or main business premises; (4) articles of association for a company, a noncompany enterprise legal person or a farmers' specialized cooperative or federation, or a partnership agreement for a partnership; and (5) other documents required by laws, administrative regulations or the State Administration for Market Regulation. A company also submits appointment documents and identification for its legal representative, directors, supervisors and senior managers. An individual business does not need articles of association. Applicants declare their own names in accordance with the law; subscribed-capital registration is the general rule, with statutory exceptions; and business scope is selected from the published classification. Complete applications in the required legal form are confirmed and registered on the spot. Otherwise registration must be completed within 3 working days, with an approved extension of another 3 working days for complex cases. The business license's issue date is the entity's formation date. These national Chinese rules took effect in March 2022.
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: State Council (2021), Regulations on the Administration of Registration of Market Entities, Order 746, Articles 10, 13, 14, 16, 19 and 21. <https://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2021/content_5636139.htm> State Administration for Market Regulation (2022), Implementing Rules for those regulations, Order 52, Articles 12, 25 and 26. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/2022-03/02/content_5676403.htm> Ministry of Finance and National Development and Reform Commission (2014), Notice on Canceling, Suspending and Exempting Certain Administrative Fees, Caishui [2014] 101, Annex 1. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/2014-12/23/content_5023513.htm> Accessible official texts: [registration regulations](https://www.samr.gov.cn/zw/zfxxgk/fdzdgknr/bgt/art/2023/art_f7eb837050cf489d9b1dba27fa502fce.html), [implementing rules](https://www.samr.gov.cn/djzcj/zcfg/gz/art/2023/art_7e27c612a1bf4be9aa6163a8cf6a13d8.html) and [fee suspension list](https://www.mof.gov.cn/gp/xxgkml/szs/201412/t20141229_2510460.htm).
- Notes: Separate the free registration procedure from an intermediary's service charge. Seal prices are set by the market; the State Council General Office's [2018 notice](https://app.www.gov.cn/govdata/gov/201805/17/424530/article.html) prohibits requiring a designated seal maker. Don't inflate capital for appearances. The contribution you subscribe is a real obligation, generally payable within 5 years for a newly formed limited liability company; see entry 3 for liability and transitional rules. For activities requiring a permit, wait until it is granted before opening; see entry 7. **Legal clarification:** 5 categories do not mean exactly 5 sheets of paper or an exhaustive list without additional requirements. On-the-spot registration depends on a complete, legally compliant application; it is not an unconditional promise of a license during your first visit. Registered capital does not cap every possible personal liability.

### 7. If the activity needs a permit, don't open before you have it
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=中 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=自由 -->

- Cost: 0 to check whether your planned activity needs a permit; obtaining it and meeting its conditions may cost money. In China, food sales and catering generally require a food business permit, subject to statutory exemptions. Selling only prepackaged food uses a filing procedure instead. Wait for any required permit before opening.
- In plain language: A business registration certificate does not replace an activity-specific permit. Operating without the required authorization can bring an order to stop, confiscation of illegal proceeds and the penalties set by the relevant law. Serious unlawful dealings in monopoly or restricted goods, or unauthorized securities, futures, insurance or payment settlement services, can be criminal offenses. Under Article 225, the lower sentencing tier includes up to 5 years' imprisonment or criminal detention and a fine of 1–5 times the illegal proceeds, or a fine alone. Don't spend on fitting out premises before checking the requirements, and don't treat a trial opening as an exemption.
- Benefit: The legally designated authority investigates activities carried out without the required permit. Article 13 of the 2017 rules provides a fallback penalty for unregistered business where no law or administrative regulation specifies a penalty: an order to stop, confiscation of illegal proceeds and a fine of up to 10,000 CNY. That is not a general ceiling for missing permits or registration today; see the clarification below. Unlawfully dealing in monopoly, exclusive or restricted goods, or conducting unauthorized securities, futures, insurance or payment settlement business, can constitute illegal business operations where the conduct violates national provisions, disrupts market order and is serious. The lower tier is up to 5 years' imprisonment or criminal detention, with a fine of 1–5 times the illegal proceeds, or that fine alone. Particularly serious cases carry at least 5 years' imprisonment plus a fine of 1–5 times the illegal proceeds or confiscation of property. These rules apply nationally in China.
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: State Council (2017), Measures for Investigating and Handling Unlicensed and Unregistered Business Operations, Order 684, Articles 5, 6 and 13. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/2017-08/23/content_5219861.htm> State Administration for Market Regulation (2023), Measures for the Administration of Food Business Licensing and Filing, Order 78, Article 4. <https://www.gov.cn/gongbao/2023/issue_10606/202307/content_6894763.html> National People's Congress (1997), Criminal Law, Article 225. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/fl/201802/t20180206_364975.shtml> Accessible official texts: [2017 measures](https://www.samr.gov.cn/zw/zfxxgk/fdzdgknr/fgs/art/2023/art_1d7d6082eec0402fa5a45a1636f1af93.html), [food business rules](https://www.samr.gov.cn/zw/zfxxgk/fdzdgknr/fgs/art/2023/art_91a91c26ae464a2f898952d5b84f62c6.html), [Criminal Law through Amendment XII](https://fgk.chinatax.gov.cn/zcfgk/c100009/c5212248/content.html) and [registration regulations, Article 43](https://www.samr.gov.cn/zw/zfxxgk/fdzdgknr/bgt/art/2023/art_f7eb837050cf489d9b1dba27fa502fce.html).
- Notes: Finance, healthcare and education have separate licensing laws. This entry checks only the general enforcement rules and criminal provision, not each sector's full requirements. Bubble tea shops and snack bars generally provide catering services and need the corresponding food business permit. Check premises requirements before fitting out the shop and obtain the permit before a trial opening. **Legal clarification:** missing business registration and missing a sector permit are different violations. Under Article 43 of the registration regulations effective in 2022, operating without required registration brings an order to correct and confiscation of illegal proceeds; refusing to correct brings a fine of 10,000–100,000 CNY, while serious cases bring closure and a fine of 100,000–500,000 CNY. The 2017 fallback fine is therefore not a universal current limit. Criminal liability also requires the statutory elements; a missing permit alone does not automatically mean imprisonment.

### 8. Keep up with tax filings after registration, even without income; use a zero return only when it fits the facts
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=中 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->

- Cost: 0 to file yourself through China's electronic tax office. A straightforward monthly or quarterly return may take a few minutes once the records are ready; follow the schedule applicable to your taxes. Hiring a bookkeeper is a separate market expense. Employers must also apply for social insurance registration within 30 days of formation.
- In plain language: No income does not automatically remove a filing obligation. File on time, reporting the actual figures; no tax due does not mean every box should contain zero. Late filing can bring a fine of up to 2,000 CNY, or 2,000–10,000 CNY in serious cases. If failing to file also causes underpayment, the tax must be paid with a daily surcharge of 0.05%, plus a fine of 0.5–5 times the underpaid tax. Missing returns for every tax you are required to report for 3 consecutive months triggers abnormal taxpayer status and suspension of tax invoice use. Unresolved tax issues can also prevent simplified closure. An employer's social insurance registration deadline is 30 days after formation.
- Benefit: Taxpayers must submit the required returns even when no tax is payable for the period. For late filing, the tax authority orders correction and may impose a fine of up to 2,000 CNY; serious cases may bring a fine of 2,000–10,000 CNY. Failure to file that causes unpaid or underpaid tax leads to recovery of the tax and surcharge, plus a fine of 50%–5 times the shortfall. The daily surcharge is 0.05% of overdue tax. Where a taxpayer with filing obligations misses all tax returns for 3 consecutive months, the administration system automatically assigns abnormal taxpayer status and suspends the invoice collection book and tax invoices. Filing the overdue returns and paying the applicable fine automatically removes that status. Employers must apply to the local social insurance agency within 30 days of formation. These are national Chinese rules.
- Evidence grade: A
- Sources: State Council (2002), Implementing Rules of the Tax Collection and Administration Law, Order 362, Articles 32 and 75. <https://fgk.chinatax.gov.cn/zcfgk/c100010/c5195082/content.html> NPC Standing Committee (2015), Tax Collection and Administration Law, 2015 amendment, Articles 25, 32, 62 and 64. <https://fgk.chinatax.gov.cn/zcfgk/c100009/c5195081/content.html> State Taxation Administration (2019), Announcement on Certain Tax Administration Matters, Announcement 48 of 2019, Part III. <https://fgk.chinatax.gov.cn/zcfgk/c100012/c5195194/content.html> NPC Standing Committee (2018), Individual Income Tax Law, 2018 amendment, Article 12. <https://fgk.chinatax.gov.cn/zcfgk/c100009/c5193028/content.html> NPC Standing Committee (2018), Social Insurance Law, 2018 amendment, Article 57. <http://www.npc.gov.cn/zgrdw/npc/xinwen/2019-01/07/content_2070267.htm> See also the accessible official [tax implementing rules](https://www.chinatax.gov.cn/chinatax/n810214/c102374/c102377/c102378/c3575330/content.html), [Social Insurance Law](https://www.samr.gov.cn/zw/zfxxgk/fdzdgknr/bgt/art/2023/art_e81d115419b4463ebb59ec46467fb136.html) and [2025 business deregistration guidance](https://www.samr.gov.cn/zw/zfxxgk/fdzdgknr/djzcj/art/2025/art_1b0f53c66d4c4daf893f7220a394ac7b.html).
- Notes: Under the integrated registration system, a separate tax registration certificate is no longer required. Use the unified social credit code on the business license to access the electronic tax office, confirm registration details and identify the applicable taxes; see the [2018 State Council notice](https://app.www.gov.cn/govdata/gov/201805/17/424530/article.html). Individual businesses and sole proprietorships pay individual income tax on business income: advance payments are due within 15 days after the month or quarter ends, and annual settlement by March 31 of the following year. Required filings can begin before trading starts; a zero return is still a return. Don't ignore 3 months of filing obligations and lose access to invoices. Unresolved tax issues can prevent simplified deregistration; see entry 19. **Legal clarification:** report actual revenue, expenses and other required figures, including tax-exempt amounts. Having no tax to pay is not permission to replace them with zeros. Follow the filing periods assigned to the applicable taxes. Abnormal taxpayer status can be removed; it is not a permanent ban on closing. Announcement 48 is marked as amended because Part IV was repealed in 2025; the Part III rule cited here remains in the published text.

### 9. Invoice only real transactions and use the small-scale VAT exemption when you qualify
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=自由 -->

- Cost: 0 to follow the rule: don't buy or sell invoices, issue them for someone else's transaction or route another company's payments through your books. Invoice and report your actual business.
- In plain language: In China, qualifying small-scale VAT taxpayers with sales below 100,000 CNY a month or 300,000 CNY a quarter can use the VAT exemption. Certain transactions normally taxed at 3% are taxed at 1% through 2027. That is legitimate relief; obtaining a false invoice to create deductible costs is not. Issuing a false VAT special invoice or another invoice used to deduct tax can be a criminal offense, but a discrepancy alone does not automatically bring a prison sentence. The type of invoice, intent, tax amount and statutory conditions matter.
- Benefit: Article 23 of China's VAT Law exempts small-scale taxpayers whose sales do not reach the threshold. From 1 January 2026 through 31 December 2027, that threshold is 100,000 CNY in monthly sales, 300,000 CNY in quarterly sales or 1,000 CNY for a taxable transaction or day. During the same period, the 3% levy rate is reduced to 1% except for the sale or rental of immovable property and transfers of land-use rights. The exemption concerns VAT; income tax is separate. Criminal Law Article 205 covers false VAT special invoices and other invoices used for export refunds or tax deductions, including issuing them for yourself or others, having others issue them for you and arranging them. On conviction, the first sentencing tier is up to 3 years' imprisonment or criminal detention and a 20,000–200,000 CNY fine; the higher tiers are 3–10 years for a large tax amount or other serious circumstances, and at least 10 years or life imprisonment for an especially large amount or other especially serious circumstances. These are national Chinese rules.
- Evidence grade: A

### 11. In a Chinese contract, distinguish 定金 from 订金 and spell out damages and payment terms

- Notes: Three recurring traps are a cartoon character or celebrity picture found online and printed on clothing, cups or phone cases; a font used commercially without the right licence; and an unlicensed asset slipped into a design commissioned from somebody else. Use a stock image only within its exact licence, keep the licence page and purchase record, or have the designer specify the sources and permitted uses of every asset in the contract. A promise by the designer to compensate you for a dispute does not automatically release the seller from liability to the rights holder. Civil damages and, when public interests are harmed and the statutory conditions are met, an administrative fine of 1–5 times illegal turnover can apply even when criminal thresholds are not met. If someone uses your own artwork without permission, the same rights framework can help you enforce it. Check trademarks before production as described in entry 15. The Chinese legal figures remain in CNY because they are statutory thresholds; they are not local US or European fines.
